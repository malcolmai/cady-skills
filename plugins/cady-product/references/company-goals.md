# Company goals

- **Goals:** Keep the company's priorities as open goal work items (submit_company_work_checkpoint with workKind "goal"). Each carries in its brief: why (the driver and its size in one line), target {count, unit, by, definition}, stopIf {below?, by?, text?}, expectedCost (context for judging it, never authority to spend) and alternative (the strongest alternative, including stopping). A goal is reviewed when loop-core's goal call says why (see the goal call reasons), not on a date. Direction keeps the priorities, constraints and bets in the owner's words; each goal is a measurable outcome serving one of them and names it in why. The set of open goals is where effort goes; never name agents, tiers or cadence in a goal, which are the orchestrator's. Starting, reshaping or stopping a goal is L1 (`cady-company-decision-check` steps 3 and 4): record it as an L1 decision with subject goal:<goalId> beside the goal write; in notify mode the owner is told, in approval mode raise that decision as an ask with your recommendation and write the goal once the owner confirms. Persistent permissions are grants, never goal fields.
- **Ideas and stops:** Follow every founder idea through as a proposed goal with its reason and the reason's size at intake. Never drop one silently; work around blockers under the blockers rule, because a blocker alone never stops a goal. Stop a goal only with its goal call (subject goal.call:<goalId>, chosen stop) carrying stop {reason, evidenceRefs (1 to 8), driverComparison (at most 400 characters)}, then close the goal: met or superseded; unexpected_cost (a non-obvious cost nobody anticipated); tradeoff_reversed (against the original driver and its size, a materially worse cost or return; time or effort alone never qualifies); one_sided_backlash (heavy, one-sided backlash weighed against actual behaviour). Before any stop other than met, run steps 3-8 on the stop itself. Goal review comes from its goal call, not a date.
