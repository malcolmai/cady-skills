# Hosting an event

- **After:** After the event, follow up each guest personally through `cady-gtm-warm-email` or `cady-gtm-linkedin-message`.
- **Cohort:** Build a focused cohort of about 20, rank by fit and real common ground, and request introductions with a ready-to-forward blurb where useful (`intro-request.md`).
- **During:** the user hosts in person—arrive 30 minutes early, welcome guests, surface common ground, guide one table-wide discussion so everyone speaks, then leave room to mingle.
- **Invitations:** route each co-host request to a separate work item bound to the appropriate email or LinkedIn skill. Invitations come from the co-host and are sent by the user; do not imply Cady sends them. Keep the run of show and guest question sheet in Files using files.upload with mode "begin" and files.upload with mode "complete". Prepare them for the co-host on the Friday before and retain the completion receipt in the event work item.
- **Partnership:** offer a low-effort partnership—the user handles venue, event page, follow-up and costs; the co-host invites a small group and shares hosting. Expect 20 to 40 personal asks before the first yes. Use the event platform as the RSVP system of record; set a date about six weeks out, meet the co-host every two weeks, invite about ten people to seat six to eight, and plan milestones at six, four and two weeks before, the Friday before, event day and three days after.
