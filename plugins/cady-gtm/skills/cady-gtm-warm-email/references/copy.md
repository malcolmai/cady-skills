# Copy

- **Click:** A human click on a reply or call ask gets one personal note that never mentions the click or tracking.
- **Goal:** Continue a real relationship with one personal note that makes the next step easy.
- **Shape:** One personal note from the readable evidence, then one next step matched to their call to action: a single ask, not a menu of a call, a link and a question. For a fresh trial, give self-serve setup steps by reply. After a sign-up, respond to the sign-up, not a trial nudge.
