# Domain method

- **Delegate:** Give each work item to one execution agent with only: Cady work <workId>: claim it, read get_cady_context {workId: <workId>}, follow it, report the result. Split only work that is chunky, needs different context, or needs independent framing; do small or tightly coupled work yourself. Start no more agents than the host and company plan allow.
- **Direction rule:** Run a learning loop, not a waterfall: hypotheses (offer, segment, trigger) -> evidence for AND against -> the cheapest test that could most change the decision -> revise offer and segment -> repeat. Conversations are one evidence type; refine hypotheses from desk evidence before any. Ladder, weakest to strongest: own reasoning, analogy and vendor-selected stories (hypothesis only) < desk evidence (public discussions, reviews, job posts, competitor positioning and pricing, communities, search) < revealed behaviour at small scale (cold-test reply or click, sign-up, ad response) < conversations about past behaviour and current alternatives, not opinions about the future < commitment (time, data, money, pilot, intro). Small-sample revealed behaviour is directional only: state the denominator, and do not mark a hypothesis supported or rejected on fewer than ~20 exposures or a single reply (underpowered). Early on prefer many cheap fast tests to one slow strong one. Never make a hypothesis a filter, stop rule or fact on weak evidence; absence of a claimed pain is evidence against it. Start from WHO and HOW; choose WHERE by purpose and constraints. Preserve evidence and unknowns.
- **Experiment guidance:** Variant shares are set in `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle`; test a channel or message with `cady-company-experiment` before scaling it.
- **Funnel review:** Review the funnel weekly with denominators, by segment and variant: sent, delivered, replied, positive replies, meetings, opportunities and customers, plus bounce and complaint rates; change the ICP or message, not the volume, when a stage underperforms.
- **Integrate:** Read results with get_company_work against the existing brief.doneWhen. For sourcing, use `cady-gtm-tam-map` completion and actual List membership evidence; keep a shortfall explicit rather than accepting a smaller or different identity unit as the requested result. Verify prepared outreach with crm.get_outreach_review before accepting work. Show prepared reviews. Release only through the skill procedure under current authority.
- **Job:** Grow demand into delivered value for the current bets. Read direction first (read_cady company:direction); it decides conflicts with this method.

### Motions

| Key | Outcome | Review boundary |
|---|---|---|
| marketing | test demand with a truthful promise and measured response | claims, publication, spend and communication |
| sales | help a buyer make an evidence-backed purchase decision; rank accounts before people | communication, commitments and consequential changes |
| customer_success | achieve expected value and recover gaps; a CRM note is not a delivery effect | communication, commitments, access and commercial changes |

- **Outreach guidance:** Select by purpose and relationship: customer cold email first touches use `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle`, email follow-ups use `cady-gtm-warm-email`, inbound replies use `cady-gtm-reply` and non-email first messages or no-reply follow-ups use `cady-gtm-linkedin-message`. Partner, organiser and supplier email messages use `cady-gtm-warm-email` even for a first approach; record that purpose without inventing engagement. Route ready recipients and copy through the matching outreach skill as a held native review, never a File. Current preparation readiness takes precedence over an older work next action; current company decisions and release holds still govern.
- **Ownership boundary:** The company's chosen CRM, Cady unless another is selected, owns relationship records. Cady owns each recipient's contact authority (party, basis, opt-outs, destination), approvals and receipts, and outreach is prepared from Cady party records; reference other external records rather than mirroring them.
- **Plan into work:** Apply the sourcing decision in role.decides through `cady-gtm-tam-map`: record N, identity type, source and channel coverage in the existing brief and brief.doneWhen before sourcing or outreach copy absorbs the work. Keep N independent of mailbox setup, send quota, grant capacity and contact eligibility. Execute sourcing alongside the next hypothesis test; integrate against the saved acceptance and report any shortfall. For sending only, cohort is the least of eligible parties, weekly company cap remaining, sender warm-up capacity, grant cap and 50; channel caps still apply. Turn each execution task into a Projects work item (submit_company_work_checkpoint: new workId, expectedRevision null, selected methodId and methodVersion, inputs in brief, acceptance in brief.doneWhen and evidenceRefs for the list or parties). One item per skill per cohort-cycle; batch message reviews up to 50 recipients and split by channel only when the skill differs.
- **Self grade:** Set objectives first; grade against receipts.

### Shared loop

- Outreach runs through the gtm skills: a cold first touch once per party per cycle in `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle` (which holds the cold email quota and variant shares); every follow-up as its own review under the follow-up rule in the outreach message rules, by the outreach or sales agent, not marketing; replies in `cady-gtm-reply` in the first run that finds them (one cadence per agent: say how soon replies are seen when the cadence is set).
