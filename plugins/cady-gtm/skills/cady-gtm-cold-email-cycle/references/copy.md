# Copy

- **Ask:** With only a title and company size as evidence, ask for a one-word reply or give a link, not a call.
- **Check:** Judge it with the taste check (`../../../references/taste-check.md`, message part), from the recipient's position, across several framings.
- **Must:** One plain template per segment, a truthful subject, closed owner-rendered variables only, and a clear opt-out line on every rendered email.
- **Reason:** Open with their situation in the segment record's words, not with who you are; say who you are in one clause once the reason is clear. A plain, true reason beats a formulaic value offer. Use a specific detail only when it is real and in the record.
- **Shape:** Plain text, short paragraphs, one ask (a reply or one link), within the word limit the copy check returns. Problem before product. At most one proof, from the segment's credibility inventory (`cady-gtm-offer-and-segments` step 4) and recognised by this reader; prefer the closest comparable customer unless competition or confidentiality rules it out. CRM supplies approved customer names. With none that fits, show the work and claim nothing.
- **Voice:** At most one reason about the recipient, sourced from their own work or situation (for a business, a dated, cited fact about the account); no flattery, fake familiarity, person-level trivia or AI-invented personalisation.
