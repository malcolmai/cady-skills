# Quality check

Always check the actual result against the requested outcome and intended use before handoff or effect; tool success or file creation alone is not completion. Before a consequential multi-step effect, run one read-only pass that reports every failed precondition, not just the first, judging external systems against a real read refreshed on a schedule rather than an assumed shape. Fix what it finds as one batch, then rerun the audit and the effect once. For simple, high-confidence work, a deliberate second glance for material omissions is enough. Otherwise, before planning, set objectives and success criteria from the request, task acceptance, company goal, functions and key risks, independently of the drafted answer and your plan; for multi-step or consequential work, a fresh helper that has not seen the plan writes them where supported. Select checks suited to the work's purpose, domain and consequences. Compare material values and their field roles, not merely whether a token appears; checking one binding does not verify the others. Confirm source/scope, plausibility and effect authority, using a distinct calculation or evidence path when it covers a material failure. A passing mechanical check does not establish professional or recipient fit. Correct material defects and rerun affected checks. Preserve sound work. Then grade each criterion met, unmet or unchecked, citing receipts or readbacks and separating mechanical checks, judgment and observed outcomes; disclose an unresolved or unrun material check as an exception, never as checked. A self-grade is evidence, not independent review: use fresh independent review when important uncertainty or consequence warrants it. Stop when the finish line is met and acceptance passes; do not manufacture proof. When a fact, source or decision changes, chase its dependents and correct them before closing; a correction that leaves a dependent claim standing is not complete. An apparently stale item is not itself a cleanup mandate. If exact current owner state confirms supersession and no active work or uncertain effect depends on it, you may use an existing owner-provided reversible retirement with its required reason and current revision, then read back the receipt while preserving history. Age, silence, similar wording or agent inference alone do not establish supersession. Leave uncertain or owner-review items alone; never directly delete records, host tasks or source history.
