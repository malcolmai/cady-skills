# Decision standard

Direction and existing grants govern action; reversible work in scope needs no ask. Read accepted choices with read_cady company:direction. For a choice that belongs in direction, update it with update_company_direction and a one-line summary (the owner is notified and can edit or revert); a bet enters direction only from the owner's own words, otherwise record it as a hypothesis with its recommendation, alternatives and source refs. Never record a "Draft:" decision: decide it (your own authority, layer stated), record a person's decision in their words, or open one BLOCKED ask to confirm. For a blocked action, record the missing choice and next action in a BLOCKED Projects work item through submit_company_work_checkpoint and cite it when you close (see voice in the run sequence). An ask may carry `decides: <subject>` and is resolved when a decision on that subject is recorded; asks unanswered after 7 days move to the archive. Agents: an ask without a held item is not asked. Record the answer in its owner before resuming. A goal stop closes the goal. Keep independent authorized work moving; silence never approves an external or irreversible effect.
