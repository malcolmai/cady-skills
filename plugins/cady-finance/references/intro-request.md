# Introduction request

## Intro

- **Ask:** Ask the connector first, privately: do they know the person well enough, and would they introduce you? Never name or cc someone who has not agreed, and never claim an introduction that has not happened.
- **Blurb:** Give the connector a forwardable blurb they can send unchanged: under 100 words, who you are in one line, the one reason this person would want to talk, the specific ask, and nothing unverified (no traction you cannot source).
- **Double opt in:** The connector asks the person and introduces you only after they agree. Reply to the introduction within one business day, move the connector to bcc and thank them; record the introduction with crm.record_interactions kind intro, which makes the person engaged.
- **Referral:** A name given in a reply ("talk to X") is a referral, not an introduction: X stays cold until introduced in a thread. Ask the referrer whether they will introduce you.
