# Outreach release

- **Permission:** Read the party with records_read party:<partyId> before drafting, including its fit, engagement and destination. Follow readiness.nextAction and act on each hold or refusal’s next call; never guess around it or clear an opt-out or suppression. An active deferral or an invalid verification blocks approval and release. Unknown verification can be approved but is held at release. For unverified destinations, use the verifier the owner names, or ask the owner which provider to use. Record a contact basis only when a governing decision states it; otherwise report the hold. The holds no_basis, sender_missing and destination_unverified block release, not preparation: prepare the review anyway.

## Preparation

Call find_cady_tools with {"detail": "schema", "tool": "crm_outreach_review_prepare"}: When exact recipients and copy are ready, claim the matching skill’s work item, then prepare the native held review. Read it with records_read review:outreach/<approvalId>. Follow current preparation readiness, even when an older next action names a different order.

## Release

- 1. Self-check: Prepare with crm_outreach_review_prepare. Every outreach review carries its relationship type (customer, investor, partner or supplier), and release puts each recipient on that board only; pass motion when a person is new to every board. Then read the review with records_read review:outreach/<approvalId>, fix refusals and prepare again. Say a message "can go out" only when no review is held; otherwise name each hold. Follow readiness.nextAction (owner_review: present the review; wait: leave release pending). 
2. Approval: Ask the person to approve: give them the review link (reviewUrl), the parties and the exact copy, in Cady or in chat; never hand the asking to someone else. Until they approve or you record their explicit approval in their exact words, the work stays OPEN awaiting approval. Outreach defaults to self-approval: the person approves the exact review in Cady, or you record their explicit approval in their exact words. You never approve alone. After the draft check and review readback, record their exact words with crm.outreach.confirm. Settings can only make this stricter; a grant the person issued can allow more. Invites, dataroom shares and grant activation still need the person in the UI. Use external sending tools only after that approval. 
3. Checkpoint: Until approved, checkpoint OPEN with nextAction 'awaiting approval of review <approvalId>', the review ref and release: true (gives up your claim). 
4. Work report: Then close the run with work_report: the run's outcome and the held review ref; the work's result waits for release.
- Own-connector send, one rule on any channel (the local LinkedIn MCP server, or your own mail tool when no Cady mailbox is connected or by choice): approve, send, note. Never send before the approve motion: a review prepared for exactly these parties and copy and approved by the person in Cady, their explicit approval recorded in their exact words (crm.outreach.confirm), or an active grant covering it, with crm.get_outreach_review mode "approved" matching what you will send. No confirmed review for a party and channel, no send. An email with a tracked link uses the review’s minted link with descriptive anchor text. Send exactly the approved text with your own tool (when readiness.nextAction is crm_outreach_release, exactly each message it returns), then record each explicit outreachOutcome with crm_interactions_create by following readiness.nextAction (its exact fields; a general note never counts as sent; never resend an uncertain send) and schedule the follow-up on each party's track with crm_tracks_set (nextAction, nextActionDueAt = send time plus the follow-up delay: the grant's followUps.delayDays from crm_outreach_grants_read, or the delayDays you prepared the follow-up with), which the due follow-ups view reads. Where Cady cannot enforce its pause or caps, readiness.nextAction reports them: hold the send while it says paused or cap reached, and hold the channel's pacing yourself.
- External CRM (company.modules crm source external): prepare with recipients (addresses) instead of partyIds, approve, then check opt-outs, send and note in that CRM; Cady records nothing.
- Server email or a device route: when approved (72 hour window), claim again, confirm crm.get_outreach_review mode "approved" (workId) matches your inputs, then crm_outreach_release {workId, expectedGeneration} (your claim fills the rest). Pass the returned ticket ids to the executor (Cady's server, or the user's cady CLI with executor=device). On RELEASE_PAUSED: keep preparing, leave release open, ask the owner to resume, and do not retry. Under an agent stop, prepare nothing more on that account and do not ask (`agent-stop.md`). Never send yourself on the server or device route; a message crm_outreach_release returns to you follows the own-tool rule. Keep receipts and reconcile any uncertain send before retrying. Report once with work_report; without release authority, say so and leave the item open.

## Outcome check

- Before any follow-up preparation or grant-covered run, detect replies with one rule per channel. Email Cady sent from a connected mailbox: first call crm_interactions_create mode capture_mailbox. Cady reads that mailbox for replies, opt-outs and bounces to mail it sent and records them, and returns the new replies by party; read only those threads for reply work. Capture files each under the provider message id (Graph: the immutable id), so recording the same message again returns already_recorded. If capture is incomplete or names a gap, triage that window with your connector before any follow-up. Record what capture cannot see (an opt-out in prose, own-connector email, LinkedIn) with mode='record_interactions', sourceRef = the provider message id.
- LinkedIn: on the account's executor (the signed-in device or browser), read each released recipient's thread and note any new inbound message and latest_outbound_at, the newest outbound message in that view; omitted means not reported, never none. Match each message to its party and approval with records_read review:outreach/<approvalId>; file it with crm_interactions_create, sourceRef = provider message id (LinkedIn: thread id plus message time); never paste a message body or quote into note.
- Kinds: reply or inbound; bounce with bounceType hard or soft and destination; opt_out with channel all, email, linkedin or marketing (destination optional: record a LinkedIn or call stop by partyId) and optOutReason complaint if so. A hard bounce suppresses its address; opt_out blocks the party on that channel scope in the same receipt; a soft bounce only records. Send one hard bounce or opt_out per call; batch replies and soft bounces up to 25. A reply or inbound message after the last release blocks follow-ups.
- Check grantsPaused for grants paused by the result; grantPausesPending pause at their next release, or pause them now with crm_outreach_grants_update.
- An anomaly-paused grant resumes only when its owner records a complete chat decision (subject grant.resume:<pause decision id>, with their quote) or approves the crm_outreach_grants_update mode='request_resume' you raise (never after a stop under `agent-stop.md`); if still over its anomaly threshold it pauses again at once.
- 'Stop', 'unsubscribe' or 'remove me' is opt_out on the channel they named, else all.
- A 'not now' is a reply: record it and set re-contact with crm_tracks_set recontact {policy: '1m'|'2m'|'3m'|'6m'|'date'}. File it; the reply is a separate work item.
- An ambiguous or positive reply is a reply, not an opt-out, and an interested lead is never dropped.
- If the mailbox or thread cannot be read, stop follow-ups on that channel and say so.

## Follow up

- Every follow-up is its own review: crm_outreach_review_prepare with followUp {delayDays, nonRepliersOnly: true}, never inside a cold cycle. Prepare it only after the outcome check finds no reply since the last release; hand a recorded replier to `cady-gtm-reply`. Under a grant, its followUps {max, delayDays} also bound count and spacing. Email follow-ups use `cady-gtm-warm-email`; LinkedIn allows one, in `cady-gtm-linkedin-message`. A follow-up to someone with no engagement on record who has not replied: at most 2 after a first touch, at least 3 days apart, each counted toward the company cold email quota. Stop on a reply, no, opt-out or suppression. A follow-up to an engaged recipient (a customer or partner, a recorded human interaction, an inbound message): paced, not counted toward the cold quota and with no count limit. For engaged recipients: follow the thread's tempo: wait about as long as they took to answer last, or 2 to 3 business days when unknown; never more than one unanswered note in 2 business days; lengthen the gap after each unanswered note. Each note adds something new or closes an open item.
- One count: Cady decides per recipient at release whether a send counts toward the cold email quota (unengaged counts, engaged does not); never pick the lane or the followUp flag to change it. Only human clicks count as engagement: attribution links record human and non-human clicks separately.
