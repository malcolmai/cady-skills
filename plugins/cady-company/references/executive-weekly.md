# Executive weekly review passes

**Point:** Score and record every goal call, initiative verdict and handover each week, so calls stay calibrated.

- **Goal calls:** Goals (`company-goals.md`): at each weekly review, call every open goal. Score its latest reading against the last call's prediction, with both numbers (predicted 10, read 6: miss). With no reading since that call, score it unknown, never hit or miss, and set a dated check that gets one; never count run reports or closed tasks as progress. Then make the call (persist, pivot, parallel or stop, per `company-goals.md`), aimed at the goal's binding constraint, and record it with work_write or decisions_write or asks_write as a decision {goalId: the goal's id, chosen: persist, pivot, parallel or stop, rationale (the reading against the prediction, the call and why, the prediction for its next reviewBy), authority: "agent", reviewBy, evidenceRefs naming the reading's source}; the subject goal.call:<goalId> and the COMPLETE status are filled in, and a new call replaces the last with supersedes [the last call's workId] and citesWhy. Record the reading itself with work_write or decisions_write or asks_write {workId: the goal's id, measure: {value, at, sourceRef}}; the reply echoes it. A stop adds stop {reason, evidenceRefs, driverComparison} to the same decision call; a goal the current bets have overtaken stops with reason superseded. Stop a goal with its stop call, never by cancelling it. On a direction change, make each open goal's call the same way; supersede or withdraw decisions that assumed the old offer, citing the new direction; close CRM tracks for audiences the new direction drops (crm_set mode track no_longer_relevant).
- **Initiatives:** Initiatives: an open initiative is a founder idea or a proposed scope expansion recorded as open work and not yet decided. Any node and any company goal may take an initiative, including goals no agent serves yet; the orchestrator and agents also start their own under their goals, so weigh theirs too. At each weekly review, weigh each one against the direction's point and bets, the binding constraint and the goals it would compete with, then decide: start it as a goal, fold it into an open goal's next attempt, or decline it with the reason, naming the trigger that reopens it when the timing rather than the idea is wrong. Record it through work_write or decisions_write or asks_write: decision {kind: "decision", authority: "agent" (omit decidedBy), subject: "initiative:<slug>", layer: "L1", chosen (start, fold or decline), rationale, reviewBy} with evidenceRefs naming the initiative's work item, and for a start the goal write beside it; then claim that item and report it done with the verdict. In approval mode, or when the verdict stays controversial, raise it under step 9 instead and report the item once it is answered. Stop an initiative that misses, with its evidence. Never add one as active work without a verdict, and never drop one silently.
- **Handovers:** Handovers and agent improvement (the outer loop). In the weekly review read the open tasks under the standing goal "Keep the system sharp"; each is an orchestrator's handover for a paused agent. Answer every one with its evidence read: change one thing through `cady-company-change-agent-behaviour` (fix the tool, owner check or instruction placement first, then one duties change, with key cases), resume it unchanged, replace it or retire it, and close the task with the answer. Improve an agent without a handover only on evidenced repeated failure, one change at a time. Hand the resume, replace or retire to the orchestrator under step 11.
- **Load and fit:** Load and fit (weekly). Read company:system_map and the people-and-agents load view when each is served (until then use the work profile reads you hold: who carries more than their stated weekly capacity, who has room, how much open work has no size). This is an allocation question, not a judgement of anyone: if someone is overloaded, name the work that should move and to whom (a person with room, or an agent), and hand that change to the orchestrator under step 11; if work is unsized, ask its assigner for a size. Where an observation shows a fit problem (the same kind of work slipping with the same holder), record it on the node as an observation and change the allocation, not the person. Strengths and weaknesses are visible only to the person and admins, are never ranked, and stay out of any note you write. Applies where people carry a work profile.
