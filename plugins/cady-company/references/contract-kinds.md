# Contract kinds

- **Backstop:** `cady-company-operations-compliance-register` acts on a change pointer for a completed contract with no contract: record. It is a safety net, not the trigger.
- **Customer contract:** Customer contract. Plan: payment terms (deposit, milestones, late payment), liability cap, data processing terms and which entity sells. Act: consumption tax registration when a threshold or a new country is crossed; invoicing and collections on the agreed terms. Illustrations: UK VAT once taxable turnover passes 90,000 GBP over a rolling 12 months; EU OSS at 10,000 EUR of cross-border B2C sales; US state economic nexus thresholds; Australian GST at 75,000 AUD; Singapore GST at 1 million SGD. Hook: `cady-sales-deal-motion` plans at the proposal and acts at won.
- **Entity or country:** New entity, office or country. Plan: where to form or register, and the intercompany agreement between related entities. Act: registration of a foreign company or branch; tax residence and permanent establishment; local payroll and filings. Illustrations: UK OS IN01 within a month of opening a UK establishment; an intercompany services agreement between a parent and its subsidiary.
- **Equity:** Founder shares, options and vesting. Plan: vesting and leaver terms, the valuation a grant needs and the approvals it needs. Act: elections with short windows on restricted equity, registers and option scheme notifications. Illustrations: US 83(b) within 30 days of the transfer; UK s431 election within 14 days, and EMI options notified to HMRC; a German GmbH share transfer needs a notary.
- **Financing:** SAFE, ASA, note or priced round; the kind with the most clocks. Plan: whether the instrument keeps an investor tax relief the round depends on; how the raise may be marketed (some exemptions forbid public promotion); founder vesting and leaver terms; that the cap table and every outstanding instrument reconcile before terms are set. Act: securities notices after the first sale; registry returns for allotted shares; foreign-investment or statistical reports when an investor is foreign; the share register and cap table; elections on restricted shares issued; investor information rights now owed. Illustrations: UK, an ASA on HMRC's terms keeps SEIS/EIS, which a standard SAFE does not, and SH01 is due within a month of allotment; US, Form D within 15 days of the first sale, state notices, BE-13 when a foreign investor takes 10% or more, and no general solicitation under Rule 506(b); Brazil, foreign capital is registered with the Central Bank. Hook: `cady-fundraise-raise-plan` plans at instrument and terms and acts at close.
- **People:** Employee, contractor or agency. Plan: worker classification on the actual working pattern; IP and confidentiality terms, explicit for a contractor; which entity engages them. Act: payroll or withholding registration; employer insurance and pension duties; right-to-work checks; the IP assignment signed before work is accepted or paid. Illustrations: commissioned work stays with its author unless assigned (UK; US outside work for hire); Germany grants exclusive usage rights rather than an assignment; UK employers' liability insurance and auto-enrolment; Singapore CPF for citizens and PRs; in Brazil a contractor (PJ) who works like an employee is a common labour claim. Hook: `cady-company-operations-people-lifecycle` plans at the offer and acts at the start.
- **Vendor commitment:** Vendor commitment: annual software, an agency or a lease. Plan: for an agency, a pilot with acceptance criteria and termination rights; auto-renewal and notice; which company account owns it. Act: renewal and notice dates in the vendor register. `cady-finance-vendor-spend-review` judges value; this kind covers terms.
