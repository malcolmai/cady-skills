# Commitment updates and takeaway routing

- **Grouping:** Samples must share a template without a personal request or actual obligation. A name or mail-merge token alone does not establish a personal exchange. Read actual prior exchanges, unresolved requests and consequential outliers individually. Narrow mixed groups rather than extrapolate from them; resample changed templates and rules 30 days old. Sampling is a cost-saving default, not proof that unread messages are safe.
- **Marketing:** Use existing inbox:marketing-treatment preferences without asking again. Keep a subscription only for genuinely useful, non-routine events or offers and actual product interest; sender targeting or mail-merge personalization is not evidence. If interest is plausible but unknown, ask once. For an approved subscription, archive and apply Marketing; surface exceptional items during this review. Do not promise ongoing monitoring without an actual scheduled duty. For irrelevant marketing, use an authorized supported unsubscribe control and archive independently. Verify each effect separately; unavailable or unconfirmed unsubscribe stays pending and must not be reported completed. Never re-subscribe without the user's choice. Actual bills, security alerts, account notices, contracts and other obligations need individual treatment even from a marketing sender; unsubscribe is not service cancellation. A promotional price or countdown is not an obligation.
- **Outcomes:** For material email, identify the underlying company outcome and current owner/work before deciding it is done or asking the user what to do. Reconcile the newest relevant correspondence with older specific dependencies; a newer newsletter or generic status may be newest by date without advancing the outcome, while manager review may supersede stale checklist wording without satisfying report receipt. Verify the actual dependency with authorized evidence. Do bounded research using available sources and existing owner methods, then give a qualified, evidence-based assessment with uncertainty and alternatives; a qualified legal assessment is useful judgment, but it is not counsel's instruction. Do not make a legal filing, instruct counsel, send or spend without the existing owner authority, review or grant. Route verified durable facts only through their canonical owner and supported fields, with source references rather than copied message content; do not invent an IP repository or use a decision record for an externally asserted fact. Preserve existing CRM fitNote content when adding a supported fact. For ongoing outcomes, update one existing owner task/responsibility with evidence, next action and justified chase trigger/date; do not create per-email tasks, a new agent/runtime, queue or blanket research. Delegate bounded specialist work in the current host run when available and expertise or time savings justify it; otherwise leave the next step on the existing owner task for its next run, without implying that saving a task starts an agent. For mailbox batches, one writer per mailbox; sample and scan outliers once for a scoped homogeneous set, use useful provider-supported label batches, avoid whole-body rereads and recounting after each batch, and honor structured retryAfter cooldowns without switching providers to evade them. Safe idempotent label retries are distinct from uncertain sends. Do not invent provider batch limits.
- **Takeaways:** Route only material takeaways, once, to the source owner using supported canonical fields and cited evidence. Routine newsletters, receipts, notifications and thanks write nothing to CRM or context. A person's stated role or employer goes to the corresponding supported crm.update_party field; do not invent a title or IP store. For a supported lasting preference, add to fitNote while preserving its existing content. “Not now” or “talk in Q1”: crm.set_track recontact policy and date, plus nextAction and nextActionDueAt for the return; a track date is not an obligation unless someone promised. A stop, even politely inside a longer reply: crm.update_party mode permission (opted_out, named lane, else all) or an opt_out interaction, then nothing else for marketing. Record an owner decision only when the owner actually decided it: checkpoint decision with subject prefix:slug, exact words as quote and source chat, interaction in evidenceRefs; externally asserted facts and hedges are not decisions. Feedback or meeting outcome: one reference-only interaction (reply, meeting or note), no stage change. A confirmed booking resolves the matching obligation with confirmation and confirmed time as result; it is not a new obligation. A claim contradicting the record changes nothing until the person states it themselves.
- **Updates:** Use optional obligation metadata on an existing task only when useful for later retrieval and faithful to evidence; keep workKind=task, the existing outcome/title and optional brief, and source-backed terms. Reuse the same task id; do not create a duplicate for metadata. A known promisor is distinct from executor; leave promisor unknown when not supported. Preserve stated versus inferred certainty and useful conditions. Date-only promises use canonical task dueDate; never invent time or timezone. Awaiting remains OPEN with waitingOn and justified chaseAt, not blocked or Needs-you without a person action. Use report_company_work as ordinary reporting with optional obligation/brief on an item; use the exact managed checkpoint route for a focused update to existing work, with current revision/generation/claim proof. On OBLIGATION_EXISTS read and attach/update that existing work. A reply, receipt or partial delivery closes only if it meets the actual promised outcome; preserve open remainder and later evidence on the same commitment. Acknowledged cancellation means execution stopped, not fulfillment, release or waiver; no release/waiver lifecycle is implied. Completed work stays terminal on replay; genuinely new promises retain explicit successor lineage. Correct erroneous completion through supported owner route or exact handoff, preserving history. Source denial stops the affected mutation; continue independent work.
