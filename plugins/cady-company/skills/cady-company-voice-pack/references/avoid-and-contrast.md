# Avoid and contrast

- Good avoid lines are quoted phrases or one character ("em dashes", "quick chat"), not a style judgement. Bad: "sound natural", "be warm", "no AI tone".
- Contrast example, same situation, owner's reply to a friend who asked about pricing. Draft: "Great question! Happy to walk you through our pricing, it's flexible, transparent and built for growth. Let me know if you have any questions!" Sent: "it's 40 a seat, 10 seats min. want the one pager?" The note names the texture difference (length, casing, no filler), not a lesson about what to say.
