# Company strategy and operating review

**Point:** Find which intended outcomes are supported, slipping or unknown, then advance the most consequential work, not every function.

**Company context:** `cady_read {"search": {"query": "<what you are about to do, in one sentence>"}}`

Review company direction, goals and progress, including onboarding a new company: its first offering and route to customers. Use when: a company needs an evidence-based operating review or onboarding. Not for: one executive decision (use `cady-company-executive-review`).

## Bets

- Choose work by consequence, the one or two problems that decide whether the company wins, and advance independent authorised work alongside. Instead of: Work a prioritised queue top to bottom, or review and initialise every function equally.
- Judge by achieved results against intended conditions and dates, not task counts, status or files created. Instead of: Report activity: open tasks, active agents and assets produced.
- At onboarding, save the owner's stated company and focus as direction early, in their words, adding nothing. Instead of: Research the company first, then write a fuller direction from what was found.
- End each review with what the next runs change (takeaways, efficiency, reliability) and a check of the review itself: did it return to the point and go deep where it decides? Instead of: Score the outcomes and list findings, leaving how runs work and how they are judged unchanged.

## Company review

- **Action:** Choose work by consequence: first the one or two problems that decide whether the company wins, then urgency, dependencies and time to usable value; every open goal is a goal record with an executing owner; a goal with none is the gap, so propose the agent. Priority is not a sequential queue: advance useful independent authorized work alongside the urgent outcome. Distinguish agent preparation from demands on people, delivery capacity, spend and provider limits. State the actual constraint when recommending deferral. Preserve healthy systems and sound decisions. Complete and verify coherent work through existing goals, task leads and chosen systems. In a proposed procedure, distinguish a verified existing owner or authorized route from one still to be selected or approved; a pending proposal is not an approved process. Name the unresolved owner decision and hold only its dependent action. Tie each unresolved condition to the action it actually prevents. Pilot delivery capacity can delay a pilot promise or commitment, while a supported example and truthful timing response can proceed under their own delivery authority. Recheck dependent copy when the condition changes. Keep dated coverage, material findings, output refs and the next trigger in the existing result/checkpoint. Carry continuation bindings forward as delivery_completion.handoff says. Report only the check coverage actually established. Preserve the previous successful coverage checkpoint after incomplete reads.
- **Assessment:** Show a compact review by relevant outcome or obligation: intended condition; dated evidence and its limits; existing owner/work; working, needs attention, unknown or intentionally deferred with a reason; next action or decision. Judge effect over activity as the Company method states it; distinguish execution progress from customer results. Do not score or initialize every function.
- **Close:** End the review with two lines in the result. Next: each change to the next runs (a takeaway, an efficiency fix or a reliability fix), naming the agent, method or goal it changes, routed as your method says. Check: whether this review judged from readings against the point, where it went deep, and what it would judge differently next time.
- **Evidence:** Start with current Company direction, intended economics, obligations and selected scope. Read relevant work_read goals, tasks and results across the company, then material domain/source evidence. An empty current-session work list or no due expectation is not absence of ready work; inspect bounded ready finite tasks, recoverable claims, agent repair and maintenance under existing authority. Follow relevant pagination or disclose incomplete coverage. Reuse sufficient context; inspect changes since the last successful review and outstanding commitments. Compare actual results and dates to intended conditions, deadlines and dependencies; task counts, active status and file creation do not establish business effectiveness.
- **Job:** Identify which intended business outcomes are supported, slipping or unknown, and advance the most consequential useful work. Use for company onboarding and an authorized operating review; the assessment leads into the work it identifies.
- **Recurrence:** Use work_read orchestration for the existing operator and schedule controls. Inspect the operating agreement, full goal binding and actual host schedule before changing recurrence. Reuse a suitable authorized review cycle and send findings to existing domain owners without competing claims. Add a separate review schedule only for a distinct authorized need with no suitable existing operator. Choose cadence from deadlines, signal arrival and acceptable evidence age; coalesce overlaps and back off empty checks. Schedule an authorized review without inventing a first-run prerequisite, and verify host readback before claiming it active. Reading setup context alone starts no schedule; an explicit business-setup remit may request routine operating review. Context QA maintains written context; it is not this business review.
- **Strategy review:** When the direction or a goal itself needs a decision, use `cady-company-executive-review`; this review supplies its operating evidence (the assessment above) and carries the decision into the next useful deliverable and its existing owner.
