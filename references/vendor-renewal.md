# Renewal, trial or cancellation

- **Cancel:** for cancellation prepare exact steps and an unsent proposal with sending account, recipient or portal route, subject, full notice body, attachments, deadline, effective date, savings and consequences. Recheck current terms, party, account and notice date immediately before presenting it. Show full proposal for exact user review and hold the external action; a standing outreach grant does not substitute for this review.
- **Commitment:** record provider role and sourced renewal date on that party through crm.update_party with its current revision and read back crm.get_party. Keep one party-linked commitment per vendor and plan in Company Work with amount, currency, cadence, trial end, renewal date, notice period and notice date, auto-renew status, owner, evidence refs, confidence and explicit unknowns. Unknown amount is not zero; annual equivalent is not booked spend. Distinguish annualized run-rate, full-term commitment and period cash exposure; include seats, metering caps, tax or FX, trial conversion and exit cost when relevant. A register line is evidence, not a reservation.
- **Notice:** calculate notice deadline from evidenced terms and renewal date, naming date uncertainty. Create a dated Company Work next action with submit_company_work_checkpoint, or update a claimed one with transition_company_work(action: 'checkpoint'), for a timely recheck; read back with get_company_work. Assess actual value and exit effects from a stated observation window, including rare critical use, before recommending cancellation. Compare realized benefit against the current solution and a credible cheaper effective workaround.
- **Party:** resolve one real CRM vendor party with crm.get_party or crm.list_parties. If needed, create it with crm.update_party with mode "create" and read it back. Read exact host terms and dated evidence, including Files text with files.read_text where applicable; do not copy payment credentials or full messages into Cady.
