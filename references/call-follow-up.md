# Call follow-up

- **Draft:** Draft the proposed follow-up in the sender's voice pack with exact recipient, channel, subject or opening, complete body, promised item and timing. Contact permission and email verification apply as in the outreach message rules (`../../references/outreach-message-rules.md`); a lane opt-out, destination suppression or active deferral holds it with the reason.
- **Match:** Match the host call note or transcript to the exact party, date and participants using crm.get_party and crm.find_communications. If the match is uncertain, hold the note for a person's resolution rather than guessing a relationship.
- **Separate:** separate what the buyer said, observed behavior, seller inference, questions, objections, commitments and unanswered items. Identify the buyer's next decision and the smallest promised item, answer or action that helps it. Preserve the source and uncertainty; friendly interest is not a commitment.
