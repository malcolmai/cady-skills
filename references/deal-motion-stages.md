# Deal motion stage detail

**Point:** Run each deal stage as written, from discovery questions to proposal, close plan, won and lost records.

- **Discovery:** Discovery call (use `cady-gtm-calls` for the sheet and the call record). The aim is to learn whether a problem worth solving now exists, not to pitch. Open with a short upfront contract: the time, the purpose, and that either side may conclude there is no fit. Ask about the situation and how they handle it today, what it costs them in their own numbers, why now, who else is affected or decides, and how they buy (security, legal, procurement, budget cycle). The buyer talks most. Show only the part of the product that meets the problem they named. End with a dated next step agreed on the call, not "I'll follow up".
- **Qualify:** Qualify lightly with four questions, each answered from the buyer's words or marked unknown: problem (real and costly to them?), priority (why now; what happens if nothing changes?), power (who signs, who else must agree?), path (the steps and dates to a decision). Unknowns become the next conversation's questions; never fill them by inference. Enthusiasm, a demo request or a trial sign-up is a signal, not qualification. No problem, owner or timing: say so and close it with crm_interactions_set: an interaction with motion customer and stage lost, its note the reason, and due the re-contact date when there is one. A fast no is a good result.
- **Proposal:** Proposal: only after the buyer agrees the problem and asks for terms; walk through it live when you can rather than emailing it cold. One or two pages: their situation and goal in their words; the outcome and how both sides will measure it; scope in and out; price from the accepted offer, at most three options with one recommended; timeline to first value; what you need from them; validity date and payment terms. A discount or non-standard term is the owner's decision; trade it for something real (term, timing, reference), never give it to get a reply. Save with files_set and read it back with cady_read file:<id>; send it through `cady-gtm-reply` or `cady-gtm-warm-email`. Before sending terms, run `cady-company-operations-contract-checks` (plan, customer contract) for payment, liability, data and tax terms. For accompanying message copy, use `outreach-drafting.md`, draft_check before `taste-message.md`; route preparation and release through `outreach-release.md`.
- **Close plan:** Mutual close plan: work back from the date the buyer needs the result, with dated steps each side owns (technical check, security review, legal, procurement, signature, kickoff). Agree it with the buyer's champion; save it with files_set. A missed buyer-owned date is the earliest sign of a stalled deal: ask about it directly, once, instead of chasing. No invented deadlines, exploding offers or pressure on a champion to skip their own process.
- **Won:** Won: close it with one crm_interactions_set call, stage won on the interaction with motion customer, its note why they bought in their words and the reason kind (pain_fit, referral, timing, price or other), and evidence (a short quote, or the crm:interaction ref of the order recorded with crm_interactions_set kind interaction (an interaction of kind note), sourceRef the order or invoice ref, no document text); name source (other:<how they came>) only when Cady never sent to them. Set role customer with renewalDate in its own crm_set call. Write a handoff note with files_set: why they bought and how they will judge success, in their words; scope and anything promised beyond standard; people and champion; first-value date; open risks and objections; contract start, renewal and notice dates. Start the customer work item with work_write {kind: task}; `cady-customer-success-lifecycle` runs it. Anything sold that the product cannot yet do goes to the product owner now, not at kickoff. Then run `cady-company-operations-contract-checks` (act, customer contract).
- **Lost:** Lost: close it with one crm_interactions_set call, stage lost on the interaction with motion customer, its note the reason in the buyer's words (mark it inferred when it is yours) and the reason kind (price, timing, no_fit, competitor, no_need, went_quiet, champion_left or other), optional evidence, and due a re-contact date the buyer gave. Correct a close with another interaction that moves the stage, its note saying why. Ask the buyer once, without pressure, what would have needed to be different, and keep their words apart from your inference. Note the stage it died at. Three or more losses with one reason are a proposed change for `cady-gtm-offer-and-segments`; one loss is not a pivot.
