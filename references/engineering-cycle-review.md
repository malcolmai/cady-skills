# Engineering cycle review, checkpoint and data paths

**Point:** Review several issues by architectural cause and checkpoint each motion once, so recurring failures become findings.

- **Architecture review:** An architecture review of several issues, by default: group them into families by architectural cause; per family, map the affected user loops from the product loop maps and owner map, current against target, as step, owner, state, refusal or recovery, next, marking open edges; trace each issue's owner, callers, state and schema, authority, cost, failure path and tests until they collapse into few root causes; name adjacent impacts on other surfaces and products, other families, in-flight work, migrations and methods; then give the target at the lowest owner that can hold it, extending canonical owners, as ordered fix slices with edge cases, non-goals and owner-only questions.
- **Checkpoint:** 8. Record each motion's result as a checkpoint on the cycle's work item: findings as Projects items citing the dimension, journey goal and evidence; for a product you build in code, place whole-outcome failures under the journey, and each other finding in exactly one owner × quality class cell using `quality-classes.md`. When findings recur under the same owner and quality class, assess whether they share an architectural cause; use that evidence to update the owner’s planning defaults. Record delivered items with the reviewed commit; the review with its scores and ranked direction. Cite each dimension and end-to-end run from its existing source in that checkpoint (dimension, test, failure signature, commit, date), deduplicated by signature. A signature that persists across three runs or three days becomes an architecture finding for the next review, not a test to re-run until its work lands. Schedule recurring motions with `cady-company-manage-agents` (`agent-hosts.md`, scheduling) under an owner binding; this skill grants no schedule, send or spend authority. End each cycle's checkpoint with the two lines of execution topic company_review close; a Next change is a Projects item under its owner like any other finding.
- **Data paths:** 9. Data paths. For a material data path (import, transformation, reconciliation or migration), identify the authoritative source and record identity; decide replay/backfill and schema-drift behavior; then reconcile the result against the business outcome and relevant quality or freshness limits. Reuse `cady-engineering-integrations` for provider idempotency. Product or the domain owner defines metric meaning; Engineering owns pipeline correctness and reports unresolved drift.
