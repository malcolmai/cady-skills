# Recording a stated outcome and a sensitive decision

**Point:** Record a person's stated outcome in their words and mark sensitive detail, so only the right people see it.

- **Stated outcome:** A new or changed outcome the person states in chat is company state before execution, even with no work item yet: record it with decisions_set {path (its node, with bet or point) | subject (a listed subject), quote: their exact quote, authority: manager (admin for an Admin)}; a goal call is work_set {id: the goal, call, note}. Read back the saved record so a later run can find it. Reuse a matching recorded answer; do not invent words, infer a grant, or ask them to repeat an outcome already supplied.
- **Sensitive:** Mark it sensitive (sensitive true, optional sharedWith) when it holds personal, HR, legal, financial-account or customer-confidential detail; only you, those shared and admins who turned on See everything then see it; everything else is company record every member reads.
