# Outcome check

Before any follow-up preparation or grant-covered run, read the sending mailbox through the host connector for released threads; crm.find_communications does not show new replies. Match each message to its party and approval with crm.get_outreach_review(approvalId); file it with crm.record_interactions, sourceRef = provider message id; never paste a message body or quote into note. Kinds: reply or inbound; bounce with bounceType hard or soft and destination; opt_out with channel all, email, linkedin or marketing (destination optional: record a LinkedIn or call stop by partyId) and optOutReason complaint if so. A hard bounce suppresses its address; opt_out blocks the party on that channel scope in the same receipt; a soft bounce only records. Send one hard bounce or opt_out per call; batch replies and soft bounces up to 25. A reply or inbound message after the last release blocks follow-ups. Check grantsPaused; grantPausesPending are paused at their next release, and you may pause them now with crm.manage_outreach_grant. An anomaly-paused grant resumes only when its owner records a COMPLETE chat decision (subject grant.resume:<pause decision id>, with their quote) or approves the crm.manage_outreach_grant action='request_resume' you raise; if the grant is still over its anomaly threshold it pauses again at once. 'Stop', 'unsubscribe' or 'remove me' is opt_out on the channel they named, else all. A 'not now' is a reply: record it and set re-contact with crm.set_track recontact {policy: '1m'|'2m'|'3m'|'6m'|'date'}. Hand every reply to `cady-gtm-reply`; an ambiguous or positive reply is a reply, not an opt-out, and an interested lead is never dropped. If the mailbox cannot be read, stop follow-ups and say so.
