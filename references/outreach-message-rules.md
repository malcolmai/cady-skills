# Outreach message rules

## Follow up

- **Count:** One count: Cady decides per recipient at release whether a send counts toward the cold email quota (unengaged counts, engaged does not); never pick the lane or the followUp flag to change it.
- **Engaged recipient:** A follow-up to an engaged recipient (a customer or partner, a recorded human interaction, an inbound message): paced, not counted toward the cold quota and with no count limit; an interested lead is never dropped. Follow the thread's tempo: wait about as long as they took to answer last, or 2 to 3 business days when unknown; never more than one unanswered note in 2 business days; lengthen the gap after each unanswered note. Each note adds something new or closes an open item.
- **Mechanism:** Every follow-up is its own review: crm.prepare_outreach_review with followUp {delayDays, nonRepliersOnly: true}, never inside a cold cycle (cold_template mode refuses it: COLD_NO_FOLLOW_UP). Prepare it only after the outcome check finds no reply since the last release; the review refuses a recorded replier (REPLIED_SINCE_RELEASE: hand them to `cady-gtm-reply`) and anyone whose last touch is newer than delayDays (FOLLOW_UP_NOT_READY). Under a grant, its followUps {max, delayDays} also bound count and spacing. Email follow-ups use `cady-gtm-warm-email`; LinkedIn allows one, in `cady-gtm-linkedin-message`.
- **Unengaged non replier:** A follow-up to someone with no engagement on record who has not replied: at most 2 after a first touch, at least 3 days apart, each counted toward the company cold email quota. Each adds something useful and new; stop on a reply, no, opt-out or suppression.
- **Outcome check:** Before any follow-up preparation or grant-covered run, detect replies with one rule per channel. Email: read the sending mailbox through the host connector for released threads; crm.find_communications does not show new replies. LinkedIn: on the account's executor (the signed-in device or browser), read each released recipient's thread and note any new inbound message and latest_outbound_at, the newest outbound message in that view; omitted means not reported, never none. Match each message to its party and approval with crm.get_outreach_review(approvalId); file it with crm.record_interactions, sourceRef = provider message id (LinkedIn: thread id plus message time); never paste a message body or quote into note. Kinds: reply or inbound; bounce with bounceType hard or soft and destination; opt_out with channel all, email, linkedin or marketing (destination optional: record a LinkedIn or call stop by partyId) and optOutReason complaint if so. A hard bounce suppresses its address; opt_out blocks the party on that channel scope in the same receipt; a soft bounce only records. Send one hard bounce or opt_out per call; batch replies and soft bounces up to 25. A reply or inbound message after the last release blocks follow-ups; the review also refuses a recorded replier (REPLIED_SINCE_RELEASE). Check grantsPaused; grantPausesPending are paused at their next release, and you may pause them now with crm.manage_outreach_grant. An anomaly-paused grant resumes only when its owner records a COMPLETE chat decision (subject grant.resume:<pause decision id>, with their quote) or approves the crm.manage_outreach_grant action='request_resume' you raise; if the grant is still over its anomaly threshold it pauses again at once. 'Stop', 'unsubscribe' or 'remove me' is opt_out on the channel they named, else all. A 'not now' is a reply: record it and set re-contact with crm.set_track recontact {policy: '1m'|'2m'|'3m'|'6m'|'date'}. File it; the reply is a separate work item. An ambiguous or positive reply is a reply, not an opt-out, and an interested lead is never dropped. If the mailbox or thread cannot be read, stop follow-ups on that channel and say so.
- **Permission:** Before drafting outreach, read crm.get_party with partyIds for up to 25 selected parties, or partyId for full tracks and recent notes. Resolve each party's marketing and non_marketing lanes, basis facts, suppression, verification, fit note and engagement. blocks: no_basis. Follow readiness.nextAction; record a missing basis with crm.apply_contact_basis {basisKind, cited source, partyIds}: relationship for a real relationship; legitimate_interest only for a business-role address with a relevant B2B offer where the jurisdiction allows B2B marketing email; consent where recorded. Apply by partyIds (up to 100) for the parties that qualify; use listId only when every eligible party in the List qualifies. Jurisdiction unknown or consent-first: apply none; exclude with the reason in the work result exclusions (a crm.update_party fitNote needs a cited source). Party-wide opt-out blocks both lanes and marketing opt-out blocks marketing. Destination suppression and active deferral block either lane; unacceptable email verification blocks email. For an unverified imported email, run the company's verifier on the host (MillionVerifier or pattern/MX evidence), then record it with crm.update_party mode verification using provider, optional checkedAt and results [{identifier, verdict, evidenceRef?}]. Catch-all is allowed but risky; invalid, disposable and unknown stay out. Cady makes no outbound verification requests. Record observed replies, meetings, calls, introductions and sign-ups with crm.record_interactions using source references only. A retry of a completed interaction call returns already_recorded per item; retrieve its receipt with crm.get_receipt, kind interactions and the identical first-call inputs. CRM derives engagement. Engaged or unverified parties never enter cold outreach. An opt-out in a note is not applied.
- **Release:** Prepare with crm.prepare_outreach_review, read crm.get_outreach_review, fix refusals and prepare again. Follow readiness.nextAction (owner_review: present the review; wait: leave release pending). In notify mode approve it yourself with crm.outreach.confirm after the verified self-check (the owner is notified); on SELF_APPROVAL_OFF the person approves. Until approved, checkpoint OPEN with nextAction 'awaiting approval of review <approvalId>', the review ref and release: true (gives up your claim), then stop with no result. Route A, a channel you send with your own connector (the local LinkedIn MCP server): approve, send, note. Never send before the approve motion: a review prepared for exactly these parties and copy and confirmed (crm.outreach.confirm, or an active grant covering it), with crm.get_outreach_review mode "approved" matching what you will send. No confirmed review for a party and channel, no send. Send exactly the approved text with your connector, then record each send with crm.record_interactions (kind note, the reviewed partyId, approvalId, occurredAt, sourceRef the connector's message id or crm:outreach_review:<id>, note uncertain when unsure; never resend) and schedule the follow-up on each party's track with crm.set_track (nextAction, nextActionDueAt = send time plus the follow-up delay, 4 days by default), which the due follow-ups view reads. Cady does not enforce its pause or caps on this route but readiness.nextAction reports them: hold the send while it says paused or cap reached, and hold the channel's pacing yourself. No ticket or crm.execute_approved_outreach for these channels. External CRM (company.modules crm source external): prepare with recipients (addresses) instead of partyIds, approve, then check opt-outs, send and note in that CRM; Cady records nothing. Server email or a device route: when approved (72 hour window), claim again, confirm crm.get_outreach_review mode "approved" (workId) matches your inputs, then crm.execute_approved_outreach {workId, expectedGeneration} (your claim fills the rest). Pass the returned ticket ids to the executor (Cady's server, or the user's cady CLI with executor=device). On RELEASE_PAUSED wait or ask an admin to resume. Never send yourself on these routes. Keep receipts and reconcile any uncertain send before retrying. Report once with report_company_work; without release authority, say so and leave the item open.

## Shared core

- **Booking:** Offer one concrete next step. Offer specific slots only from the sender's real availability in the evidence (a calendar read or the owner's stated times); otherwise send the sender's booking link if one is recorded, or ask the owner for times in the review notes. Never invent the sender's times. If the recipient asked for information, provide it without adding a call.
- **Claims:** Make every commercial claim precise and supportable within the product's current capability and evidence. Do not promise a result, imply customer approval, turn a hypothesis into a fact or use proof that is not real and relevant.
- **Deliverable:** The message or sheet contains only what the recipient reads. Report unavailable steps, missing tools and status to the person outside the deliverable.
- **Easy yes:** One small, specific, low-risk next step proportionate to the value and relationship, answerable in a word; no call when an answer, item or self-service step is enough. Say what happens after yes and leave an easy no. No hard sell: no false urgency, flattery, fake familiarity, presumptive close or invented fascination.
- **Evidence selection:** Choose details that explain why this message is useful now: a real conversation or clear company fact. Leave out profile trivia, old public items and anything that adds little.
- **Format:** Plain text: no markdown, bold or headers, and no em dashes. Short paragraphs, one purpose, a subject that matches the body; no placeholders, stock openings, jargon or internal process. On LinkedIn write as people chat: a few short messages, one thought each; never split one thought or merge replies. A connection note is one message. Emoji only where the sender's voice pack or the thread makes it natural.
- **Framing:** Start from the recipient's situation, not the product, with one idea. Aim for recognition ("that's true for us") or a non-obvious, truthful reframe: surprising, simple, elegant, nothing to remove, nothing to decode. Draft several framings and pick by the recipient test. Judge concept (is this reason worth their attention?) apart from execution; polish cannot rescue an empty concept, and simple must not strip useful substance.
- **Opt out:** Lanes. Marketing (lane "marketing", an opt-out line on every email, which the review checks): cold first touches, follow-ups to unengaged non-repliers, and any promotional or bulk email. A one-to-one warm note to an engaged contact also goes on the marketing lane, but its opt-out is one plain closing sentence in the sender's voice (for example "If you'd rather I didn't follow up, reply to opt out and I'll stop." with the words opt out or unsubscribe), never an unsubscribe block or footer. Non-marketing (lane "non_marketing", no opt-out line, not blocked by a marketing opt-out): only a reply in a live thread. Until the review checks a contract basis, a contractual or legal notice and an investor update go on the marketing lane with the opt-out line; if a recipient has opted out, hold that send for the person, who decides. Never clear or route around a recorded opt-out or suppression (contact permission enforces it). If outreach release is paused, prepare anyway; only the release waits.
- **Sender voice:** Write in first person as the named sender. Read their voice pack with crm.manage_voice_pack (mode read) before drafting and write in it; without one, use senderLabel in a clear natural register. The owner approves example messages and personalisation rules on the grant, not a voice description. Imply no opinion absent from the record.
- **Truth and grounding:** Use only facts supported by current company, recipient or conversation evidence. Prefer the closest readable source and check its date and scope. Separate direct observation from interpretation; leave unknowns out rather than filling gaps with plausible detail. Do not invent familiarity, experiences, results, customer names, product capability, commitments or promises. Name a price, product step, address or asset only when the evidence contains it; otherwise point to where it lives or offer to send it.
- **Unknowns:** An empty, refused or "0 searched" tool result is not known: write "not searched" or "unavailable", never "no prior contact". Resolve a relative date ("Tue") from the date of the note containing it, and flag when it may have passed.
