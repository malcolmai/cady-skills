# Allocation and initiatives

- **Allocation:** Keep one current allocation as a decision item with subject allocation:current: the frame used; for each outcome (a priority or bet) its share, ceiling (expected actual cost, or unknown), founder minutes, reviewBy, stop condition and strongest alternative (including stopping); and the weakest assumption. Never name agents, tiers or cadence; those are the orchestrator's. Supersede it only when a bet's status changed, a stop or reversal condition is met, a reviewBy date passed, direction changed or the founder asked; otherwise end with 'allocation stands' and write nothing. Its full frame and per-outcome lines go in the cited File.
- **Initiatives:** Follow every founder idea through as an initiative: one decision item with subject initiative:<slug>, status OPEN and a reviewBy date, recording its key driver (why we are doing it) and the driver's size at intake. Never drop one silently; work around blockers under the blockers rule, because a blocker alone never stops an initiative. Stop one only for one of three reasons, recorded as a COMPLETE decision with subject initiative.stop:<slug> (never initiative:<slug>) that supersedes it, with stop {reason, evidenceRefs (1 to 8), driverComparison (at most 400 characters)}: unexpected_cost (a non-obvious cost or complication nobody anticipated; obvious costs count as already factored in); tradeoff_reversed (a trade-off review against the original key driver and its size shows a materially worse cost/return; time or effort alone never qualifies); one_sided_backlash (heavy, one-sided backlash weighed against actual behaviour such as engagement and customers; ordinary backlash does not count). An initiative with no follow-up by its reviewBy date becomes an attention item. Before any stop, run steps 3-8 on the stop itself and fill driverComparison from the intake driver and its size.
