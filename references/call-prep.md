# Call prep: the call sheet

**Point:** Ground each call in the person's situation and the accepted offer, so it earns an honest next step.

Include an opener with name, role and one line; either a permission-based opening or a short upfront contract; honest intent; mini-SPIN with one segment-specific situation, problem, implication and need-payoff question; qualification criteria taken from the accepted offer; and next-step options of a 15-minute call or information by email. Ground every claim in supplied evidence.
