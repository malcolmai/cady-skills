# Cold email deliverability

- **Pause:** Pause rules: stop cold sending from the affected mailbox at once, and pause any active grant with crm.manage_outreach_grant action 'pause', when a send shows hard bounces above 2%, spam complaints above 0.1% (0.3% is the large mailbox providers' ceiling, not a target), provider blocks or rate-limit codes (550 5.7.x, 421 4.7.x), a blocklist listing, or the host's reputation tool showing low or bad. A flagged mailbox: keep only one-to-one replies on it; find the cause (stale list, volume jump, broken authentication, links or images, complaint-prone copy); fix it; wait for one to two weeks of clean normal mail; then ask the owner to resume (action: 'request_resume') at half the previous volume. Never switch to a fresh domain or mailbox to keep sending the same list. Tell the owner what tripped and the evidence.
- **Setup:** Check the sender before the first cycle and after any change; Cady cannot see DNS or mailbox reputation, so the user or host checks and you record what was shown. Domain: cold mail goes from a secondary domain that redirects to the main site, at least two weeks old to protect the main domain. Authentication: SPF lists only the real senders (one record, under 10 lookups); DKIM signs with the From domain (2048-bit); DMARC exists with reporting, p=none first, quarantine once aligned. Verify on a test send to a seed mailbox that SPF, DKIM and DMARC all show pass. Warm-up: a new mailbox sends normal human mail for two to four weeks first, then cold at about 10 a day, rising by about 10 a day each week to 30 to 50 per mailbox at most; add mailboxes rather than push one harder.
