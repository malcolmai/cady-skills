# Commitment updates and takeaway routing

**Point:** Keep each promise and decision from the inbox moving under its owner, so none is lost among bulk mail.

- **Outcomes:** For material email, identify the underlying company outcome and its current owner and work before deciding it is done or asking the user what to do. Reconcile the newest relevant correspondence with older specific dependencies: a newer newsletter or generic status does not advance the outcome. Verify the actual dependency with authorized evidence, then give a qualified, evidence-based assessment with uncertainty and alternatives; a legal assessment is judgment, not counsel’s instruction. Route verified durable facts only through their owner's supported fields, with source references rather than copied message content; preserve existing CRM fitNote content when adding to it. For an ongoing outcome, update one existing owner task with evidence, next action and a justified chase date; never per-email tasks, new agents or queues. For mailbox batches, use one writer per mailbox, sample a homogeneous set once, and honour structured retryAfter cooldowns without switching providers.
- **Updates:** Keep obligation metadata on the existing task (same id, workKind task, its outcome and title) and only as evidence supports: a known promisor is distinct from the executor (leave it unknown when unsupported), stated and inferred certainty stay distinct, and a date-only promise uses dueDate with no invented time. Awaiting stays OPEN with waitingOn and a justified chaseAt. On OBLIGATION_EXISTS, update that work. A reply, receipt or partial delivery closes it only if it meets the promised outcome; acknowledged cancellation means stopped, not fulfilled. Completed work stays terminal on replay; a genuinely new promise is a successor.
- **Takeaways:** Route only material takeaways, once, to the source owner's supported fields with cited evidence; routine newsletters, receipts, notifications and thanks write nothing. A stated role or employer goes to crm_parties_set or crm_parties_update; a lasting preference is added to fitNote, preserving it. A "not now" or a stop follows `outreach-release.md`, outcome check. Record an owner decision only when the owner decided it (their exact words as quote, source chat, the interaction in evidenceRefs); asserted facts and hedges are not decisions. Feedback or a meeting outcome is one reference-only interaction with no stage change; a confirmed booking resolves its obligation. A claim contradicting the record changes nothing until the person states it.
- **Marketing:** Use existing inbox:marketing-treatment preferences without asking again; if interest is plausible but unknown, ask once. Keep a subscription only for genuinely useful events or offers and real product interest; otherwise use an authorized unsubscribe control and archive. Verify each effect; an unconfirmed unsubscribe stays pending. Never re-subscribe without the user's choice. Bills, security alerts, account notices and contracts need individual treatment even from a marketing sender; unsubscribe is not cancellation.
- **Grouping:** Sample only messages that share a template with no personal request or obligation; read real prior exchanges, open requests and consequential outliers individually. Sampling saves cost; it does not prove unread messages safe.
