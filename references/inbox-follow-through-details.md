# Commitment updates and takeaway routing

- **Takeaways:** Route only material takeaways, once, to the source owner. Routine newsletters, receipts, notifications and thanks write nothing to CRM or context. A person's stated role or employer: crm.update_party (role in its own call, companyName in another; no title field, so title goes in fitNote). Lasting preference: fitNote, preserving existing content. “Not now” or “talk in Q1”: crm.set_track recontact policy and date, plus nextAction and nextActionDueAt for the return; a track date is not an obligation unless someone promised. A stop, even politely inside a longer reply: crm.update_party mode permission (opted_out, named lane, else all) or an opt_out interaction, then nothing else for marketing. An owner decision (for example, “we will not do paid ads”): checkpoint decision with subject prefix:slug, exact words as quote and source chat, interaction in evidenceRefs; a hedge is not a decision. Feedback or meeting outcome: one reference-only interaction (reply, meeting or note), no stage change. A confirmed booking resolves the matching obligation with confirmation and confirmed time as result; it is not a new obligation. A claim contradicting the record changes nothing until the person states it themselves.
- **Updates:** Use optional obligation metadata on an existing task only when useful for later retrieval and faithful to evidence; keep workKind=task, the existing outcome/title and optional brief, and source-backed terms. Reuse the same task id; do not create a duplicate for metadata. A known promisor is distinct from executor; leave promisor unknown when not supported. Preserve stated versus inferred certainty and useful conditions. Date-only promises use canonical task dueDate; never invent time or timezone. Awaiting remains OPEN with waitingOn and justified chaseAt, not blocked or Needs-you without a person action. Use close_company_work as ordinary reporting with optional obligation/brief on an item; use the exact managed checkpoint route for a focused update to existing work, with current revision/generation/claim proof. On OBLIGATION_EXISTS read and attach/update that existing work. A reply, receipt or partial delivery closes only if it meets the actual promised outcome; preserve open remainder and later evidence on the same commitment. Acknowledged cancellation means execution stopped, not fulfillment, release or waiver; no release/waiver lifecycle is implied. Completed work stays terminal on replay; genuinely new promises retain explicit successor lineage. Correct erroneous completion through supported owner route or exact handoff, preserving history. Source denial stops the affected mutation; continue independent work.
