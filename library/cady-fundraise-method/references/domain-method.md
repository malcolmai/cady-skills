# Domain method

**Point:** Decide whether, when and how much to raise for named milestones, so every list and material serves one plan.

- **Delegate:** Give each work item to one execution agent with only: Cady work <workId>: read cady_read work:<workId>, follow it, report the result. Split only work that is chunky, needs different context, or needs independent framing; do small or tightly coupled work yourself. Start no more agents than the host and company plan allow.
- **Integrate:** Read each result with cady_read and inspect the exact CRM list or Files refs before relying on it. Send the helper one corrected prompt on a miss. Investor contact and update sends go through `cady-fundraise-investor-outreach`; present the copy as unsent until its review and authority are satisfied. Before adding a cap-table row, verify the investment against its evidence document and add it only after funds are received. A SAFE is not issued shares. Correct with a dated correcting row; the file's generations are its history.
- **Job:** Help the company decide whether, when and how much capital to raise for named milestones. Read the root's point and bets (cady_start carries them in its `node` and `above` fields) to decide whether to raise at all. Distinguish internal strategy, investor fit, materials and contact authority. Make each round a node below finance/raise (decisions_set {create: "finance/raise/<round>", point: what the round is}, with its first bets as items of the same call; a tree without finance/raise gets it below finance the same way first), record its instrument, target and lead as bets on that path, keep its terms in a round file under fundraising/ in Files that the bets cite, and set each investor's round party field to the round's node key. The cap table is the versioned CSV legal/cap-table.csv in Files (holder, class, quantity, consideration, date, evidence file). Verify investment evidence before adding a cap-table row; a SAFE is not issued shares.
- **Plan into work:** Turn each commissioned investor list or material into one Projects work item with work_set {kind: task, path, headline, evidenceRefs: source refs, next, brief {doneWhen}}: the server mints the id and takes the method from the path. Do not create an unrequested round or bundle of materials.
- **Round review:** Review the round against its plan each cycle: read investor pass reasons back, and read a flat or negative response by the "Separate causes before concluding no demand" bet in `cady-company-evidence`.
