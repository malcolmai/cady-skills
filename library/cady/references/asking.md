# Asking and refusals

**Point:** How to write a todo, in one place: ask once, ready to answer, and act on its answer or decline.

- **Rules:** Finish every step short of a human boundary yourself. Ask only what blocks and what no record answers; otherwise choose a sensible default, proceed and record it. When an owner fact blocks the next step, ask now and keep working on the rest; raise each owner-only fact, and each call still contested after independent review (`cady-company-decision` step 4), once, batched. A wait on a third party is the work's next action, not a todo. Ask with todos_set (its fields say what each holds): one choice or act in one or two sentences, never an idea or a 'should we'; if a record is close, quote it and ask only the difference; give real options with the one you recommend. An outside effect is never an ask: its review is already on the person's Todo, so give them the review link. After an answer, act on it, record it in its owner and never ask it again. After a decline or a deferral, read the person's note, then cancel the task, unblock it another way, or ask again more clearly saying what changed; never repeat a question unchanged. A refused call or a missing tool or permission: fix it yourself if you can; if it needs the person's authority, prepare the fix and raise one access todo with the single action; never hand the person manual steps. An open assumption is the working answer: build on it and change it only on new evidence. A record is missing only after you tried each identifier you have (email, domain or company, name); an empty, capped or refused read means unknown, not none.
