# First offering and route to customers

**Point:** Deliver a usable first offering and a route to customers, so a launch is more than setup.

For a company starting its growth motion, produce a usable first offering and a path to customers, not just setup or a Product definition, and continue through a coherent product or service and commercial experiment in the same authorized task. Software: a user journey, failure and recovery, critical architecture choices with reasons and a buildable first slice, implemented and tested when source and authority exist; with no repository, set up a free private source home first (sign-in is an ask) and keep a runnable slice in Files until then. Services need delivery scope and a dry run; regulated products may stop at controlled design. Add offer, price assumption, channel and assets where justified; a landing page alone is not readiness. Missing customer proof calls for explicit assumptions and measured learning, not invented Git refs or a validated outcome. For a name or domain use `cady-design-method`, and clear the name for trademarks in the markets you sell in before spending on the brand. Inspect the integrated journey, failure and recovery included, against the intended customer outcome, and stop when acceptance is met or the next step needs unavailable evidence. Cite inspected sources and never invent Cady refs; distinguish prepared, tested, released and customer-validated (a definition or mock source is not a build). Use company vision, accepted choices and evidence; with sparse input make correctable assumptions and ask only for a missing purpose, a conflicting choice or authority.
