# Decision standard

Direction and existing grants govern action; reversible work in scope needs no ask. An agent starts or stops an initiative (sub-goal or task) under its signed goals with a reason, target and stop condition in one checkpoint; a call crossing goals, adding cost or needing another agent goes to the orchestrator. What governs is the chain of points and bets to the work's node: cady_start returns the node's point and up to 8 bets on its chain (180 characters each), decision:<id> reads one item, and company:direction gives the company root's point when it is needed. Only a choice that changes where resources go or how functions work together goes on the tree, as a point or a bet (we do X, not Y, because W, with its basis): decisions_set at its node's path (`cady-company-decision-check` step 6). An agent replaces a bet by its basis and cited evidence, and a point or an owner-decided bet is an ask (`cady-company-decision-check` step 7); an override grants no outside-effect permission. Narrow knowledge goes to its owning home (product map, test or check, method, work result). Priorities name their goals; stage belongs to company:stage. Route an owner-only missing fact to one ask, an answer the agent can research to a task, and strategic uncertainty to a hypothesis bet. Unknowns and copied source lists are not company-context fields. Never record a "Draft:" decision: decide it (your own authority, layer stated), record a person's decision in their words, or open one BLOCKED ask to confirm. For a blocked action, record the missing choice and next action on the work you hold (work_set {id, status: blocked, next, note}) and cite it when you close (see voice in the run sequence). An ask may carry `decides: <subject>` and is resolved when a decision on that subject is recorded; asks unanswered after 7 days move to the archive. Agents: an ask without a held item is not asked. Record the answer in its owner before resuming. A goal stop closes the goal. Keep independent authorized work moving; silence never approves an external or irreversible effect.
