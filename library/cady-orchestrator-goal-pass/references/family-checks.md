# Family checks

**Point:** Gate each family's effort on its own evidence, limit or lead time, so effort goes where delivery breaks.

Apply the row for the goal's family, then decide inside the family's node by its point and bets and record each call as `cady-company-decision` step 6 says; a call that crosses families is yours (step 7), and one that would change a point, bet or goal goes to the Executive with both options and the evidence. The function's own skills do the work; this table holds only the gate.

| Family | Gate (owner skill) | Trigger events | Report line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GTM | Evidence gate, for a stalled goal or a segment under its reply line only (a moving goal above its line is left alone): that segment goes back to positioning, never to more volume; clear live replies before any new send; route effort to the weakest funnel stage (`cady-gtm-pipeline-pass`). | a reply, a segment crossing its reply line, a stage stalling | stage counts per segment, the weakest stage, where effort went, the calls made |
| Product | WIP limit: hold new starts while work in progress is over its limit; an item that cannot name what it will change waits; a release closes on a fresh user's story walk, and its results return to discovery (`cady-product-manage`). | a release due, work in progress over its limit, results arriving | work in progress against its limit, items held and why, releases passed or held, what was learned |
| Finance | Runway line, in order: gate each commitment against runway, passing one that does not move the line in this pass with a one-line reason, never as an ask (one that moves it goes to the Executive); check the last period is closed; set the funding start backwards from the line with slack. A met standing line is persisted, never closed and replaced; a missing funding timeline goes in the report line, an ask only when the line is breached (`cady-finance-runway`). | a new commitment, a period ending, the runway line moving | runway, the close state, commitments gated, the funding timeline |
| Operations | Lead time: start each obligation when its lead time opens, not at its deadline; track each inbox commitment to done or an explicit decline (`cady-company-operations-method`). | an obligation's lead time opening, a commitment found in the inbox | obligations started, those at risk, commitments open, done or declined |
