# Domain method

- **Delegate:** Give each work item to one execution agent with only: Cady work <workId>: claim it, read context_read {workId: <workId>}, follow it, report the result. Split only work that is chunky, needs different context, or needs independent framing; do small or tightly coupled work yourself. Start no more agents than the host and company plan allow.
- **Direction rule:** Run a learning loop, not a waterfall: hypotheses (offer, segment, trigger) -> evidence for AND against -> the cheapest test that could most change the decision -> revise offer and segment -> repeat. Conversations are one evidence type; refine hypotheses from public sources (reviews, forums, job posts, competitor pricing) before any. Weigh each line by reliability (factual, reputable, anecdotal, inferred) and by how widely it holds for the question at hand: many agreeing anecdotes can outweigh one factual survey of the wrong group; behaviour about the past beats opinions about the future; commitment (time, data, money, pilot, intro) beats both. Small-sample behaviour is directional only: state the denominator, and do not mark a hypothesis supported or rejected on fewer than ~20 exposures or a single reply (underpowered). Early on prefer many cheap fast tests to one slow strong one. Never make a hypothesis a filter, stop rule or fact on weak evidence; absence of a claimed pain is evidence against it. Plan from the persona's profile, written first as `cady-gtm-persona-profile` says, not from the company's own records and calendar. Then choose HOW, and WHERE by where they look, purpose and constraints. Preserve evidence and unknowns; logging a call or answering a question records what was said, and its bearing on a hypothesis waits for the segment review (`cady-gtm-offer-and-segments`).
- **Funnel review:** Review the funnel at each cold cycle's mid-cycle and end reads (`cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle`), with denominators, by segment and variant: sent, delivered, replied, positive replies, meetings, opportunities and customers, plus bounce and complaint rates; change the ICP or message, not the volume, when a stage underperforms.
- **Integrate:** Read results with work_read against the existing brief.doneWhen, at two levels (two_level_review, `cady-company-taste-review` step 4): why first, whether the work serves a real buying need and the goal, assumptions named; then the details. For sourcing, use `cady-gtm-tam-map` completion and actual List membership evidence; keep a shortfall explicit rather than accepting a smaller or different identity unit as the requested result. Verify prepared outreach with records_read review:outreach/<approvalId> before accepting work. Show prepared reviews. Release only through the skill procedure under current authority.
- **Job:** Grow demand into delivered value for the current bets. Read direction first (records_read company:direction); it decides conflicts with this method.

### Motions

| Key | Outcome | Review boundary |
|---|---|---|
| marketing | test demand with a truthful promise and measured response | claims, publication, spend and communication |
| sales | help a buyer make an evidence-backed purchase decision; rank accounts before people | communication, commitments and consequential changes |
| customer_success | achieve expected value and recover gaps; a CRM note is not a delivery effect | communication, commitments, access and commercial changes |

- **Outreach guidance:** Select by purpose and relationship: customer cold email first touches use `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle`, email follow-ups use `cady-gtm-warm-email`, inbound replies use `cady-gtm-reply` and non-email first messages or no-reply follow-ups use `cady-gtm-linkedin-message`. Partner, organiser and supplier email messages use `cady-gtm-warm-email` even for a first approach. Route ready recipients and copy through the matching outreach skill as a held native review, never a File. Current preparation readiness takes precedence over an older work next action; current company decisions and release holds still govern.
- **Ownership boundary:** The company's chosen CRM, Cady unless another is selected, owns relationship records. Cady owns each recipient's contact authority (party, basis, opt-outs, destination), approvals and receipts, and outreach is prepared from Cady party records; reference other external records rather than mirroring them.
- **Plan into work:** Apply the sourcing decision in role.decides through `cady-gtm-tam-map`, which holds N, identity type, coverage and acceptance. Execute sourcing alongside the next hypothesis test; integrate against the saved acceptance and report any shortfall. For sending only, cohort is the least of eligible parties, weekly company cap remaining, sender warm-up capacity, grant cap and 50; channel caps still apply. Turn each execution task into a Projects work item (work_create: new workId, expectedRevision null, selected methodId and methodVersion, inputs in brief, acceptance in brief.doneWhen and evidenceRefs for the list or parties). One item per skill per cohort-cycle; batch message reviews up to 50 recipients and split by channel only when the skill differs.
- **Self grade:** Set objectives first; grade against receipts.

### Shared loop

- Outreach runs through the gtm skills: a cold first touch once per party per cycle in `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle` (which holds the cold email quota and variant shares); every follow-up as its own review under the follow-up rule (`../../../references/outreach-release.md`, follow_up), by the outreach or sales agent, not marketing; replies in `cady-gtm-reply` in the first run that finds them (one cadence per agent: say how soon replies are seen when the cadence is set).
