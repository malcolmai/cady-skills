# Domain method

- **Delegate:** Give each work item to one execution agent with only: Cady work <workId>: claim it, read cady_read work:<workId>, follow it, report the result. Split only work that is chunky, needs different context, or needs independent framing; do small or tightly coupled work yourself. Start no more agents than the host and company plan allow.
- **Direction rule:** Run a learning loop, not a waterfall: test offer, persona and message assumptions against evidence for and against, using conversations, public sources and other evidence; choose the cheapest test that could most change the decision, then revise and repeat. Keep the few pieces of evidence that drove each decision, each with where it was read (cady-gtm-persona-profile), and say how widely it holds in one honest phrase rather than labelling or counting sources; past behaviour outweighs opinions about the future; commitment of time, data, money, a pilot or an introduction outweighs both. State the denominator: fewer than about 20 exposures or one reply is directional, not enough to support or reject an assumption. Early on prefer many cheap fast tests to one slow strong one. Never turn weak evidence into a filter, stop rule or fact; absence of a claimed pain is evidence against it. Plan from the persona's profile, written first as `cady-gtm-persona-profile` says, not from the company's own records and calendar. Then choose HOW and WHERE by where they look, purpose and constraints. Preserve evidence, sources and unknowns; how that evidence bears on an assumption is decided at the persona review (`cady-gtm-offer-and-segments`).
- **Funnel review:** Review the funnel at each cold cycle's mid-cycle and end reads (`cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle`), with denominators, by persona and variant: sent, delivered, replied, positive replies, meetings, opportunities and customers, plus bounce and complaint rates; change the persona or message, not the volume, when a stage underperforms. Read a flat or negative response by the "Separate causes before concluding no demand" bet in `cady-company-experiment`.
- **Integrate:** Read results with work_read against the existing brief.doneWhen, at two levels (two_level_review, `cady-company-taste-review` step 4): why first, whether the work serves a real buying need and the goal, assumptions named; then the details. For sourcing, use `cady-gtm-tam-map` completion and actual List membership evidence; keep a shortfall explicit rather than accepting a smaller or different identity unit as the requested result. Verify prepared outreach with cady_read review:outreach/<approvalId> before accepting work. Show prepared reviews. Release only through the skill procedure under current authority.
- **Job:** Grow demand into delivered value for the current bets. Read direction first (cady_read company:direction); it decides conflicts with this method.

### Motions

| Key | Outcome | Review boundary |
|---|---|---|
| marketing | test demand with a truthful promise and measured response | claims, publication, spend and communication |
| sales | help a buyer make an evidence-backed purchase decision; rank accounts before people | communication, commitments and consequential changes |
| customer_success | achieve expected value and recover gaps; a CRM note is not a delivery effect | communication, commitments, access and commercial changes |

- **Outreach guidance:** Select by purpose and relationship: customer cold email first touches use `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle`, email follow-ups use `cady-gtm-warm-email`, inbound replies use `cady-gtm-reply` and non-email first messages or no-reply follow-ups use `cady-gtm-linkedin-message`. Partner, organiser and supplier email messages use `cady-gtm-warm-email` even for a first approach. Route ready recipients and copy through the matching outreach skill as a held native review, never a File. Current preparation readiness takes precedence over an older work next action; current company decisions and release holds still govern.
- **Ownership boundary:** The company's chosen CRM, Cady unless another is selected, owns relationship records. Cady owns each recipient's contact authority (party, basis, opt-outs, destination), approvals and receipts, and outreach is prepared from Cady party records; reference other external records rather than mirroring them.
- **Plan into work:** Apply the sourcing decision in role.decides through `cady-gtm-tam-map`, which holds N, identity type, coverage and acceptance. Execute sourcing alongside the next assumption test; integrate against the saved acceptance and report any shortfall. For sending only, cohort is the least of eligible parties, weekly company cap remaining, sender warm-up capacity, the standing approval's daily cap and 50; channel caps still apply. Turn each execution task into a Projects work item (work_write {kind: task, path, headline, next, brief with the inputs and doneWhen, evidenceRefs for the list or parties}). One item per skill per cohort-cycle; batch message reviews up to 50 recipients and split by channel only when the skill differs.
- **Self grade:** Set objectives first; grade against receipts.

### Shared loop

- Customer outreach runs through the gtm skills: a cold first touch once per party per cycle in `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle` (which holds the cold email quota and variant shares); every follow-up as its own review under the follow-up rule (`../../../references/outreach-release.md`, follow_up), by the outreach or sales agent, not marketing; replies in `cady-gtm-reply` in the first run that finds them (one cadence per agent: say how soon replies are seen when the cadence is set).
