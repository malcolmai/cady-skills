# GTM channel comparison

**Point:** Choose channels the persona already uses and trusts, so a first signal arrives within the stage's time and cost.

- **Choice:** Compare channels on who they reach, cost and time to a signal, so the mix fits the persona and stage. Rank first by where the persona already looks and whom they trust (the persona File's how-they-buy section), then by time to first signal against the goal date, the cost the stage can carry (money, founder hours, reputation or account risk), and learning speed. A channel the persona does not use loses however cheap it is. With a dated goal still at zero, lead with channels needing no new setup or grant that can signal inside the window; start slow-setup channels in parallel only when cheap and not competing for the same founder hours. Budget each channel's weekly founder hours and show the plan's total fits the hours the root's point and bets allow; a plan that does not fit drops a channel.
- **Experiment:** Run one or two channels at a time with pre-registered tests, stop signals and review dates, and use the GTM domain method's small-sample rule for evidence strength and the next move.
- **Boundary:** Name the angle and the customer's words it rests on; the channel's execution work drafts the copy. Write no message copy, subject line, post text or send step, even when asked what would be sent: copy here skips the drafting check and review and fixes text before the brief exists. Name no send date, follow-up cadence, cap or pause threshold; channel strategy owns those choices.
- **Cold email:** Cold email tests quickly whether strangers care, once the sender is warmed (cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle).
- **Warm network:** Warm networks can yield trusted early conversations quickly, but their small, biased sample may not generalise (`cady-gtm-warm-email`).
- **LinkedIn:** LinkedIn messages reach active buyers personally at limited volume, with risk to the sender account (cady-gtm-linkedin-message).
- **Community:** Communities reveal buyer language and build trust where contribution is welcome, but require time and respect for group rules (`cady-marketing-post`).
- **Events:** Events can build trust through deep conversations when buyers attend and the date and cost fit the goal (`cady-gtm-events`).
- **Content:** Content can compound reach among buyers who search or follow a topic, but needs distribution and time to signal (`cady-marketing-post`).
- **Launches:** Launches produce a short burst of attention whose audience may not include the persona.
- **Paid:** Paid channels can test demand quickly but cost money for every signal, within a Finance ceiling.
