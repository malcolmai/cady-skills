# Domain method

### Content system

#### Frameworks

- **Avoid when:** Do not restart work, make layers gates, or treat effort, polish or popularity as audience value. Do not force a hook, story or teaching formula.
- **Decision:** Which content and distribution work will improve demand, and how should the agent operate it?
- **Evidence bar:** Direct questions or behaviour and first-party patterns outweigh trends; creator examples are inspiration. Preserve refs and distinguish fact, inference and unknown. Known details are anchors, not a creative ceiling; resolve materially misleading claims before publication. Voice belongs to the company/person. Separate exposure, consumption, response and business outcomes; retention shapes are clues, not causes. Scope learning to conditions; use belief_evidence_loop for material revisions. Platform tactics need current evidence.

#### Execution

- Replace interchangeable advice with a supported comparison or worked example from actual customer/product evidence. For 'AI saves time', show the task, correction and artifact; without proof, narrow the promise. Identify audience access: polish alone supplies no distribution advantage.
- Operate within the agreed outcome, resources and authority. Use the installed `cady-engineering-integrations` skill for relevant integrations/recovery, and get_company_work for continuation or recurrence; reads create no schedule or access. Choose the company's systems.
- For content help, start with one or two buyer-value strategies, one main channel, about two useful pieces a week and a four-week calendar of dated or relative topics. Name the owner; after four weeks, review qualified response and effort to decide what to keep or change. An evidenced capacity or source constraint can justify less.
- Keep the plan and assets as a Files document referenced from the Projects work checkpoint, with audience and competitor evidence refs. Frame a content A/B as a bounded market test with its variants identified in the checkpoint. Cady retains material strategy and continuation refs; external systems retain their records.
- Automate stable collection; retain judgment for relevance and allocation. Monitor meaningful changes, choose cadence from the work, resume unfinished work and back off when nothing useful is ready. Return artifacts, verified effects and the next decision; preparation, scheduling, publication and impact remain distinct.

#### Questions

- Direction: preserve supplied work; resolve the consequential open decision. Which buying situation, progress, alternative and differentiated value connect to the business outcome? Load customer_job or positioning_context only if unresolved. Content competes with other interventions.
- Contribution: what do customer behaviour, questions, lost decisions and product observations support? Create original proof through demonstration, comparison, analysis or customer outcomes. Join relevance, distinctive value and renewable sources; competitor/search gaps are hypotheses, not demand. For a material content-specific comparison, reuse an existing neutral Competitors CRM list and exact source refs where present; an empty list does not establish absence or require list creation. Compare relevant formats, hooks, claims and audience response only when this changes the content decision. A narrow copyedit does not reopen competitor research.
- Distribution: choose each piece's job independently of format. Reach, teach, prove and convert are lenses, not a funnel. Give channels distinct roles; reuse evidence through native variants and recognizable themes. Earn access through search, communities, relationships or partners. Diagnose offer, proof or destination when attention fails to convert.
- Structure: make the subject and supported reason to care clear without assuming the reader knows the request. Choose the explanation, comparison, steps, story or demonstration that fulfils that promise. Advice needs an executable move or decision criterion. Remove adjacent material that dilutes the contribution; preserve needed evidence and qualifications. Align spoken and visible beats.
- Make: choose text, graphics, audio or video for the job; the founder chooses human, assisted or generated realization for each component, on your recommendation. When writing or revising an actual text component, use the written-content reference. Judge it with the taste check (../../../references/taste-check.md, post part) before review. Select the actual primary artifact and include the text component for mixed media. Reuse current company/person writing references or load the relevant canonical sources; the method does not imply they were read. Design supplies broader visual direction.
- Learn: inspect audience value, fulfilled promise, claims and natural voice. Diagnose strategy, source, structure, production, distribution or conversion before changing format. Compare matched work against account baselines; retain windows, denominators, costs and missing coverage. Fix obvious weaknesses; experiment when consequential ambiguity remains.

- **Rule:** Frameworks are scoped perspectives, never universal truth, authority or personalities to imitate. Owner evidence controls. Prefer directional learning criteria over false precision. Select for the decision, including unlisted methods. ACTIVE status or populated fields cannot validate hypotheses. Preserve sound accepted choices unless the request or evidence warrants revision. Questions and outputs guide the work; they are not required record fields. Advice alone needs no persisted state; retain material decisions through their existing owner when warranted. For CUSTOMER outreach, use the matching `cady-gtm-cold-email-cycle`, `cady-gtm-warm-email`, `cady-gtm-reply` or `cady-gtm-linkedin-message`; otherwise match the motion's described use case. When seeking commercial advantage, show which decision changes versus the credible current approach, why it could work here and the next useful action. Useful operating basics need not be novel. Distinguish practitioner observation, your adaptation, a local hypothesis and observed improvement; local improvement alone does not prove causal lift or superiority to peers. Do not turn this judgment into a mandatory score, research phase or experiment for every task.

### Seo indexing

- For an indexing defect, establish the exact intended public URL and inspect its response status and redirects, robots crawl rules, HTML or HTTP noindex, canonical, rendered content and relevant internal links or sitemap. Crawl permission and index eligibility differ: a robots-blocked crawler cannot read a page's noindex, and robots.txt is not a privacy control. Preserve deliberately private or excluded routes. Use authorized Search Console URL Inspection when available, fix the evidenced cause and compare before/after behavior. A verified technical repair is not proof of recrawl, indexing, ranking or qualified demand. Primary procedure: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro .
- Before changing robots rules, check crawler-specific matching and precedence against the intended URL and neighbouring excluded URLs. An unanchored path is a prefix, not an exact-page match; use an end anchor where exact scope is intended, and decide query-string and trailing-slash variants from the actual public URL contract. Verify positive and negative examples before claiming that exclusions are preserved. Matching reference: https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/robots-txt/robots-txt-spec .
