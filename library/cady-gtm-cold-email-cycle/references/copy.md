# Copy

- **Ask:** With only a title and company size as evidence, ask for a one-word reply or give a link, not a call.
- **Check:** Judge it with the taste check (`../../../references/taste-check.md`, message part), from the recipient's position, across several framings.
- **Must:** One plain template per segment, a truthful subject, closed owner-rendered variables only, and a clear opt-out line on every rendered email.
- **Reason:** Say honestly what is happening and why it may matter to this person; with no specific shared context a plain line is right, and a plain reason beats a formulaic value offer. Use a specific detail only when it is real and in the record.
- **Shape:** Plain text, short paragraphs, one ask (a reply or one link), within the word limit the copy check returns. Problem before product. Use relevant, permissioned proof of the same problem or outcome; prefer the closest comparable customer unless competition or confidentiality rules it out, then use the nearest non-competing one. CRM supplies approved customer names. Omit proof when none fits.
- **Voice:** At most one sourced account-level reason (a dated, cited fact about the account that explains why now); no flattery, fake familiarity, person-level trivia or AI-invented personalisation.
