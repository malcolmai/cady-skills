# Engineering cycle dimension register and research

**Point:** Name MECE dimensions with one owner, question and label each, and research only the differentiating ones.

- **Register:** Name high-level, mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive dimensions that every goal crosses, each with one question, owner and boundary. For software the usual questions follow. They are a start, not a required set: name one only where the product has code of its own for it, fold the rest into the nearest one as a part, split an Interface question per client and no other, and add the product's own. Interface: how do people reach it and act; how do agents and other programs call it. Infrastructure: what does it run on; how does it reach the outside services it uses; where is each fact stored; how does the stored shape change; how is stored data found; who is calling; how is money taken (the provider's calls and its records; plans and prices are Business logic; one file that holds both needs two owners: see `engineering-system-map.md`); how is the running system seen; where do its agents or background jobs run. QA: how does a change ship without breaking what is live; how do we know a change did not break the code; how do we know a user can still reach each goal. A QA question is owned code (the checks and what runs them), not the quality of being verified. Before users or revenue, name only the 3 to 5 a goal actually crosses. With users, expect 8 to 14 by that test; the length of the list is not a target. For a product you build in code, keep folded owners as one-line homes; every code area has exactly one owner, including a module’s SQL, pages and tests. Do not register qualities such as cost, isolation, verification or efficiency as owners; use `quality-classes.md`. For other products, choose dimensions from how the offering is made, delivered and used; fold inactive dimensions into the active owners as decisions. Scope, ICP and pricing live on the tree and in decisions, not the register. Keep only what has its own owner, question and test; add one only for a new subsystem, region, provider or buyer category. Draw the map as `engineering-system-map.md` says. Where applicable, keep authentication separate from permissions, agent action authority separate from user access, and agent management under its own owner. Name the commercial category a buyer would compare. Label each differentiating or not on the owner's node in the map, with its reason. Only a real choice (a binding rule, or a standard-tool choice such as "use Stripe plainly") is recorded (a bet at the product node with decisions_set, or a test or check for a rule); raise it to Product as an ask (asks_set) with your recommendation, and never record a draft label.
- **Research:** Depth follows the label. A non-differentiating dimension takes the most reliable widely used approach with the least custom code; deviation needs a named reason. A differentiating dimension gets a reviewed architecture; apply the Product card’s depth judgement on the decisive problem from the first session, including before users or revenue. Add recurring weekly scans only after the first users or revenue; before then research only where that judgement calls for it. Research names the problem space, who does the best work in it now and their latest published method, and maps it to the dimension's goal as concise do and don't guidance; the gap is the current design against that. Group differentiating dimensions N to 1 into research tracks where their commercial solutions are linked, and scan each track weekly with `cady-product-research` (its technology and competition references) for material improvements since the last scan; keep the scan as a File cited from the track's work item, and test any promising idea with `cady-company-evidence` before it changes the architecture.
