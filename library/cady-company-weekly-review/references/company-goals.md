# Company goals

**Point:** Keep each priority a measured goal with a timely persist, pivot or stop call, so effort follows evidence, not momentum.

- **Goals:** Keep the company's priorities as open goal work items (work_set {kind: goal}; its brief fields say what each holds). Each goal is a measurable outcome serving one of the root's points or bets, named in why; never name agents, tiers or cadence in a goal. The set of open goals is where effort goes.
- **Goal call:** When a plan runs out, record a reading on the goal, then make the goal call (work_set {id, call}): persist while the approach has a credible path and the next attempt adds information; pivot after two attempts fail the same way; run a parallel approach when it is cheap, reversible and not exclusive; stop only under the stop rule. Call it at each weekly review, sooner on an event. Widen before you narrow (broader persona scope, more sources, another channel), and route around a blocker in parallel. With no reading above zero yet, find a credible path to the first one before planning for the target; no credible path means change the approach, not the volume.
- **Ideas and stops:** Follow every founder idea through as a proposed goal with its reason; never drop one silently, and a blocker alone never stops a goal. A stop carries its reason and evidence (the stop field says which), and time or effort alone never qualifies; before any stop other than met, test the stop itself as a consequential call (`cady-company-decision` step 3). A stop other than met or superseded goes to the owner. After a stop, cancel the goal's obsolete tasks and archive its obsolete todos; keep unresolved effects held. An initiative well beyond an existing goal is one task for the Executive with its reason, cost and the goal it extends.
