# Agent review

Critical: review the effects of its first released cycle, then a sample; pause on any anomaly. Standard: after its first task, review only on a signal (owner correction, refusals or retries, missed or empty runs, a cost spike, a claim without readback).
Evidence: host transcripts, run records, receipts and artifacts, cited host:transcript:<lowercase uuid>, never bodies; a missing transcript means unknown, not healthy, and a trend needs two comparable windows. Count each done, live or released claim with no readback as unsupported.
Judge work by what it changed for its goal and by the rigour of its decisions (the chain from the goal, the losing alternative, current premises, owner-only calls left to the owner), not by activity or records written. On an agent that is not up to scratch, pause it and file one handover task under the Executive's standing goal "Keep the system sharp", naming the agent, the evidence refs and what was paused; you never rewrite its brief, methods or skills, and improving an agent is the Executive's (`cady-company-executive-review` step 15).
Resume a paused agent unchanged only if the cause was external, with evidence.
