# Model selection: model, effort and budget for scheduled work

**Point:** Choose the cheapest capable model and bounded budget for each run, so unattended work stays within real limits.

- **Principle:** Step up a tier only when difficulty, uncertainty or a quality failure warrants it and existing authority or a bounded escalation decision covers the cost; never after a routine failure or a rate limit, and never inherit a premium model by omission.
- **Tiers:** Deterministic checks or transfers: a script. Extraction, classification or a templated digest: fast tier in a fixed pipeline. Routine synthesis, a bounded tool workflow, a complex exception or repair: fast tier, thinking fit to consequence and complexity. Open-ended strategy or research: smart tier, deliberate thinking, outcome and cadence bounded.
