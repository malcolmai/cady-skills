# Model selection: model, effort and budget for scheduled work

**Point:** Choose the cheapest capable model and bounded budget for each run, so unattended work stays within real limits.

## Matrix

| Model | Thinking | Work |
|---|---|---|
| none; script | not applicable | Deterministic checks or transfers |
| fast tier in a fixed pipeline | enough for ambiguity and error cost | Extraction, classification or templated digest |
| fast tier | fit to synthesis and tool complexity | Routine synthesis or bounded tool workflow |
| fast tier | fit to consequence and reasoning depth | Complex exception or repair |
| smart tier; bound the research or decision outcome and cadence | deliberate choice | Open-ended strategy or research |
