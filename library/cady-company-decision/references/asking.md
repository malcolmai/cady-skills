# Asking and refusals

**Point:** How to write a todo, in one place: ask once, ready to answer, and act on its answer or decline.

- **Rules:** Finish every step short of a human boundary yourself and checkpoint at natural boundaries. Ask only what blocks and what no current record answers; otherwise choose a sensible default, proceed and record it. When a missing owner fact blocks the next step, ask now and keep working on the rest; raise each owner-only fact, and each call that stays controversial after testing and independent review (`cady-company-decision` step 5), once, batched per `cady-company-decision` step 4. A wait on a third party is the work's next action, not a todo. Write it with todos_set (its fields state their limits): question: the one choice or act you need from this person, in one or two sentences, never an idea or a 'should we'; if a record is close, quote it and ask only the difference; detail goes in a File cited by evidenceRefs. kind: what the person does: decision (a choice, the default), access (grants a login or permission), spend (approves money), send (approves outgoing copy), sign (signs a document; never recommended), call (a conversation) or physical (an act in the world). options: real answers, each with impact saying what you will do if it is picked, and recommended, the one you would pick; check the reply names the option you meant. Omit options only for a plain yes or no: Cady then stores Yes / No for a decision and Approve / Decline for the other kinds. minutes: your honest estimate of the person's time. unlocks: what answering unlocks and the cost of waiting, as a statement. readyRefs: what the person reads to answer; the first web link becomes the todo's main button (Connect for access), so an access todo carries its single action there. blocks names the work this todo holds (its own item); goal puts the question to that goal's director; for names the member asked. Prepared material under review is already on the person's Todo as its review: raise no todo to approve it and give them the review link; a todo that cites a review (readyRefs review:<family>/<id>) is approved at the review, never answered. After an answer: the held work reopens with the answer in its next action, and answeredAsks lists it on your next run. Act on it, record it in its owner and never ask it again. 'Handled elsewhere' means verify it, and raise the todo again only if the check fails. After a decline or a deferral: answeredAsks marks it declined or deferred and the held work reopens; read the todo (cady_read) for the person's note. Re-review that task and decide one of three: cancel it (work_set {id, status: cancelled, note} citing their note), unblock it another way you can take yourself, or ask again more clearly with a new todo that says what changed since the decline. Never raise the same question unchanged; a deferral is no for now. A person's answer in chat is todos_set {id, answer, quote} (a decline is {id, declined, quote, reason}); a reworded question is {id, question}. A todo you raised and no longer need: todos_change {archive: [{ref: "todo:work:<its workId>"}], reason}; that cancels the todo, not the work it holds, so move that work yourself. A refused call or a missing tool, connector or permission: fix it yourself if you can; if it needs the person's authority, prepare the fix and raise one access todo that carries the single action; if the right fix is unclear, raise one todo with the key options and your recommendation. Never hand the person manual steps. An open assumption is the working answer: treat it as answered, build on it, cite it, and propose a change only with new evidence. A record is missing only after you tried each identifier you have (email, domain or company, name); an empty, capped or refused read means unknown, not none.
