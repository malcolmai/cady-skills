# Recording a decision: fields, stated outcomes and sensitive detail

**Point:** Record each decision with its fields in its one home, so a later run can find and trust it.

- **Fields:** A strategy item is a point (what the node is for) or a bet; decisions_set says what a bet is and where it goes. Write it with decisions_set {path, bet {chosen, insteadOf, why, measure} or point}, the path being the node's keys from the top level (a path from the map in your cady_start read). Structure is an entry of the same call: decisions_set {create: <path>, point} adds a node only where work needs decisions its siblings should not inherit, {retire: <path>, into?} retires one (into takes its bets and work), and items mix them with bets in order, all or none. Narrow knowledge goes to its owning home, not the tree: product behaviour to the product map, an enforceable rule to a test or check, function practice to a method, a one-off to the work result. A goal change is the goal write itself (work_set {kind: goal} or {id, brief}, its reason in brief.why); a goal call, a stop included, is work_set {id: the goal, call, note, stop}; a fact a page reads by subject is decisions_set {subject, chosen, rationale}, never strategy. Its rationale is the chain from the goal (goal, use case or pain, emphasis, choice), the impact, the losing alternative and the premises checked with their dates, citing the one or two pieces of evidence that decided it. For a consequential call write the full record (the frame, outside view, prediction and review) as a File through files_set mode begin_upload then files_set mode complete_upload, and cite file:<fileId> in the work result (work_set {id, status: done, result, evidenceRefs}); keep the result short and the File full.
- **Sensitive:** Default to not sensitive. Mark it sensitive (sensitive true, optional sharedWith) only when others seeing it would truly be harmful; it is then invisible to everyone without access. Everything else is company record every member reads.
- **Correct:** To correct a recorded decision, replace it (decisions_set {path, bet {chosen, insteadOf, why}, replaces: <id>}; a by-subject record uses replaces with citesWhy) or rule it out (decisions_set ruleOut {id, why}; decisions_set {subject, status: wrong, why} records 'Ruled wrong: ...' on the subject). The owner is shown an agent's change; a replaced or ruled-out bet is reverted in one call, a point by writing the next point. Replacing a bet grants no outside-effect permission: it never creates or widens a grant, a person-approval category, a payment or a reserved release step, and never undoes an owner's stop (initiative.stop or a goal stop). An owner's correction of an agent's work is evidence for the next run: fix the work and record the correction (replaced or ruled out when it rules a decision wrong). With a node in your cady_start read, the work_set that finishes the work also sends observations [{nodeKey: that node, text: their words, sourceRef: the decision ref}].
