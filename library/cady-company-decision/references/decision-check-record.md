# Recording a decision: fields, stated outcomes and sensitive detail

**Point:** Record each decision with its fields in its one home, so a later run can find and trust it.

- **Fields:** A strategy item is a point (what the node is for) or a bet: we do X, not Y, because W, with its basis (validated: a reading or two independent runs; hypothesis: accept if A, reject if B, after doing C; owner decided: their words; or resource constraint) and the measure that would settle it, at the highest node it is fully relevant to. Write it with decisions_set {path, bet {chosen, insteadOf, why, basis, measure} or point}, the path being the node's keys from the top level (orchestrator.paths in your context read). Structure is its own call, never beside a bet or point: decisions_set {node: {create: <path>, point}} adds a node only where work needs decisions its siblings should not inherit, and {node: {retire: <path>}} retires one. Narrow knowledge goes to its owning home, not the tree: product behaviour to the product map, an enforceable rule to a test or check, function practice to a method, a one-off to the work result. A goal change is the goal write itself (work_set {kind: goal} or {id, brief}, its reason in brief.why); a goal call, a stop included, is work_set {id: the goal, call, note, stop}; a fact a page reads by subject is decisions_set {subject, chosen, rationale}. The rationale is the chain from the goal (goal, use case or pain, emphasis, choice), the impact, the losing alternative and the premises checked with their dates, quoting its reason from a goal, a dimension or the user, and citing the one or two pieces of evidence that decided it. A person's decision carries their exact words (quote, authority manager or admin) and, in the rationale, your inference of why they chose it then, marked as inferred; your own choice carries authority agent, which Cady records as your agent, never as the person. For a consequential call write the full record (the frame, outside view, prediction and review) as a File through files_set mode begin_upload then files_set mode complete_upload, and cite file:<fileId> in the work result (work_set {id, status: done, result, evidenceRefs}); keep the result short and the File full.
- **Sensitive:** Mark it sensitive (sensitive true, optional sharedWith) when it holds personal, HR, legal, financial-account or customer-confidential detail; only you, those shared and admins who turned on See everything then see it; everything else is company record every member reads.
- **Correct:** To correct a recorded decision, replace it (decisions_set {path, bet {chosen, insteadOf, why}, replaces: <id>}; a by-subject record uses replaces with citesWhy) or rule it out (decisions_set ruleOut {id, why}; decisions_set {subject, status: wrong, why} records 'Ruled wrong: ...' on the subject). The owner is shown an override and can revert it in one call (a takeover of a work-row decision is final: replace it with a new bet). An override grants no outside-effect permission: it never creates or widens a grant, a person-approval category, a payment or a reserved release step, and never undoes an owner's stop (initiative.stop or a goal stop). An owner's correction of an agent's work is evidence for the next run: fix the work and record the correction as theirs (replaced or ruled out when it rules a decision wrong). With orchestrator.node served, the work_set that finishes the work also sends observations [{nodeKey: that node, text: their words, sourceRef: the decision ref}].
