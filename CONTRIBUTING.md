# Contributing

Pull requests are welcome. Edit the skill under `library/` (or the front door under `skills/cady/`)
and say what the change improves.

These files are generated from Cady's own method source and rewritten on every release. When a pull
request is merged, the maintainers port it to that source and credit you, so your change survives the next release.

Keep company-specific content out: no company or person names, email addresses or private links.
Company facts belong in Cady, not in a skill.
