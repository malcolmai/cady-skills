# Contributing

This library is the upstream baseline that companies fork. Edit your company's own skills in your fork; open a
pull request here only for a change that would help every company, and say what it improves and the evidence.

Make the change in your fork, on a branch off the upstream release tag your fork is based on (its `BASELINE`),
so the diff holds only that change. Edit the skill under `library/` (or the front door under `plugins/cady/skills/cady/`).

These files are generated from Cady's own method source and rewritten on every release. When a pull request is
merged, the maintainers port it to that source and credit you, so your change survives the next release and reaches
every fork at its next monthly upstream check.

Keep company-specific content out: no company or person names, email addresses or private links.
Company facts belong in Cady, not in a skill.

## Changing your fork's guidance

Follow the skill `cady-company-change-agent-behaviour`: place the rule at its one home, classify the change,
test one nearby case, guard it and record the decision.
