# Contributing

Your company's skills are the copies it adopted into its host: edit those in place for company-specific methods.
Open a pull request here only for a change that would help every company, and say what it improves and the evidence.
Edit the skill under `library/` (or the front door under `plugins/cady/skills/cady/`), on a branch off the release tag.

These files are generated from Cady's own method source and rewritten on every release. When a pull request is
merged, the maintainers port it to that source and credit you, so your change survives the next release and reaches
every company that reads the changelog.

Keep company-specific content out: no company or person names, email addresses or private links.
Company facts belong in Cady, not in a skill.

## Changing your company's guidance

Follow the skill `cady-company-change-agent-behaviour`: place the rule at its one home, classify the change,
test one nearby case, guard it and record the decision.
